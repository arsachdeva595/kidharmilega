The 30-second verdict

Yes, if you are a qualified physiotherapist; this isn't a business you can start without a BPT or MPT degree. It needs little capital, demand from post-surgery, elderly and injury patients is steady, and good therapists get referrals quickly. The limit is travel time, so income grows through packages, clustered appointments and eventually a small team.

Market reality 2026: why now, or why not now?

Tailwinds

  • India's population is ageing, and more people need rehab after surgery or a stroke.
  • Desk jobs, gym injuries and sports have created steady demand for pain and posture treatment.
  • Hospitals discharge patients earlier, and families prefer rehab at home over daily hospital visits.
  • Physiotherapists are coming under formal registration through the 2021 allied-health law, which makes registered therapists easier to trust.

Headwinds and traps

  • You need a recognised physiotherapy degree (BPT or MPT); this isn't a business you can run without one.
  • Booking apps take a large share of the fee for each patient they send.
  • Travel between home visits limits how many patients one therapist can see.
  • Patients often stop coming as soon as the pain eases, before treatment is finished.

What changed in 2026

Home physiotherapy has become normal in Indian cities, helped by apps and hospitals that discharge patients sooner. In 2026 the independent therapists who do best have their own referral network of surgeons and hospitals and track patient progress in writing, which keeps patients coming until treatment is done.

Families increasingly prefer rehab at home after surgery or a stroke, especially for elderly patients. Apps have made home physiotherapy common but take a large cut of each visit. Independent therapists who build their own network of orthopaedic surgeons, hospitals and elder-care providers keep more of the fee and get patients who complete their treatment.

The takeaway for a 2026 bootstrapper

Build relationships with three to five orthopaedic surgeons and a hospital discharge team, cluster your appointments by neighbourhood, and sell treatment packages instead of single sessions.

Licences and government support

Licences you'll need

  • A recognised BPT or MPT degree
  • Registration with your state physiotherapy council or allied-health council where it is in place
  • Professional indemnity insurance
  • Shops and Establishments registration for a clinic
  • Healthcare services by qualified practitioners are generally exempt from GST; confirm your case with a CA

Government support worth checking

  • Udyam registration: Free, online, Aadhaar-based MSME registration. Most MSME benefits, including priority-sector bank loans, start here.
  • PM Mudra Yojana: Collateral-free bank loans in four tiers: Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishore (up to ₹5 lakh), Tarun (up to ₹10 lakh) and Tarun Plus (up to ₹20 lakh, for borrowers who repaid a Tarun loan).
  • Stand-Up India: Bank loans from ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore for new businesses started by women or SC/ST founders.

Scheme limits change. Confirm current terms on the official portal or with your bank before applying.

The hard truths: why most attempts fail in year one

  • Travel caps your income

    Every hour in traffic is an hour you can't bill. Group patients by area and time of day, and charge more for visits outside your core area.

  • Patients drop off early

    Many patients stop once the pain eases, which hurts both their recovery and your income. Sell packages of sessions, set goals in writing and show progress.

  • Collecting payment

    Collecting cash after each visit leads to missed payments. Take payment in advance for packages, and use UPI.

  • Growing beyond yourself

    Once you hire other therapists, your reputation depends on their work. Standard protocols, patient feedback and regular reviews are needed before you add a second person.

The 90-day execution blueprint

The aim is ₹1 lakh a month in sessions and a steady stream of doctor referrals.

  1. Days 1–15

    Set up

    • Check your registration status and buy indemnity insurance.
    • Buy portable equipment for the conditions you treat most.
    • Set your fees against clinics and apps in your area.

  2. Days 16–45

    Build referrals

    • Meet 10 orthopaedic surgeons, GPs and hospital discharge teams.
    • Offer a free first assessment for referred patients.
    • Set up a Google Business profile and ask early patients for reviews.

  3. Days 46–75

    Fill your calendar

    • Sell 10- and 20-session packages with written goals.
    • Group appointments by neighbourhood.
    • Run a free posture camp in an office or housing society.

  4. Days 76–90

    Review the numbers

    • Track sessions per day, completion rates and income per hour.
    • Drop areas that take too long to reach.
    • Decide whether to open a small clinic or hire a second therapist.

Frequently asked questions

Can I start a home physiotherapy service without a degree?

No. You need a recognised BPT or MPT qualification, and registration with your state council where it is in place. Unqualified treatment can harm patients and has legal consequences.

How much does home physiotherapy cost patients?

Home sessions typically cost ₹500–1,500 in Indian cities, depending on the city, the condition and the therapist's experience.

How much does it cost to start?

Portable equipment, insurance and basic branding cost ₹50,000–1.5 lakh. A clinic costs ₹10–25 lakh.

How do home physiotherapists find patients?

Mostly through referrals from orthopaedic surgeons, hospital discharge teams, elder-care services and satisfied patients, plus a well-reviewed Google Business profile.
