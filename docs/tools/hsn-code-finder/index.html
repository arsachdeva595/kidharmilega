    HSN code: common questions

    What is an HSN code?

    HSN (Harmonized System of Nomenclature) is the worldwide product code system. The first 6 digits are the same in every country; India adds 2 more digits, making an 8-digit code. You need it for GST invoices, GST returns, shipping bills and to look up trade data.

    How many HSN digits do I need on a GST invoice?

    Under CBIC notification 78/2020: if your turnover last year was up to ₹5 crore, print at least 4 digits on B2B invoices (optional on B2C). Above ₹5 crore, print at least 6 digits on every invoice. Exporters use the full 8 digits on the shipping bill.

    Where do I find the GST rate for my HSN code?

    GST rates were restructured from 22 September 2025, so use the official CBIC rate page (cbic-gst.gov.in, 'GST Goods and Services Rates') and search by the 4-digit heading. If you are unsure, ask your CA before you invoice.

    What if two codes look right for my product?

    Pick the one that describes what the product is made of and how it is made, not what it is used for. When in doubt, look at what similar sellers use on their invoices or ask a customs broker; a wrong code can mean the wrong GST rate or a held shipment.

    Is the 6-digit code the same abroad?

    Yes. The first 6 digits are global, so use them on sites like uktradeinfo, UN Comtrade or a foreign buyer's import data. Countries add their own extra digits after that.
