PM Vishwakarma Yojana Online Apply 2024 (Updated November 2026): CSC Steps, ₹15,000 Toolkit, ₹3 Lakh Loan and the 18 Trades

You still apply for PM Vishwakarma Yojana through a CSC, using Aadhaar biometrics and a mobile OTP, and it costs nothing. This November 2026 guide covers the 18 trades, the ₹15,000 toolkit, the ₹3 lakh loan at 5%, the ODOP crafts that qualify, the official helpline and where West Bengal stands now.

Short answer: you don't fill in the PM Vishwakarma Yojana form at home. You go to your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC), and the operator registers you through the official PM Vishwakarma portal login using your Aadhaar biometrics and a mobile OTP. Registration is free. If you searched "pm vishwakarma yojana online apply 2024", the process hasn't changed. One thing has, though, and this guide is current to 2 November 2026. The national target of 30 lakh registrations has been met. The official portal says it was reached in September 2025, and news reports in September 2026 covered the same milestone. Before you spend a day on it, ask your CSC, your District Industries Centre (DIC) or the helpline (1800 267 7777) whether new registrations are still being accepted.

Yeh page tumhare liye hai if you're a carpenter, potter, goldsmith, tailor, stone carver or someone in one of the other 18 traditional trades, and you want the ₹15,000 toolkit plus up to ₹3 lakh in cheap credit to grow your work into a proper local business.

What is PM Vishwakarma Yojana?

PM Vishwakarma is a central government scheme run by the Ministry of MSME. It was launched on 17 September 2023 with a budget of ₹13,000 crore for FY2023-24 to FY2027-28. It's meant for self-employed artisans in the unorganised sector who work with their hands and tools in one of 18 family-based traditional trades. The aim is to give them recognition, skills, better tools, cheap credit and an introduction to digital payments.

It turned three on 17 September 2026. The MSME Ministry held the anniversary event in Kolkata, and MSME Minister Jitan Ram Manjhi attended, according to NewsOnAir.

So the headline figure is up to ₹3 lakh of credit plus ₹15,000 worth of tools. The digital incentive pays up to ₹100 a month. That's small, but it builds a transaction history, and that history helps when you go back to a bank later. The full rules are in the official scheme guidelines (v30.0, PDF).

The 18 trades covered

You qualify because of your trade, not because of the product your district is known for. These are the 18 trades:

  1. Carpenter (Suthar/Badhai)
  2. Boat maker
  3. Armourer
  4. Blacksmith (Lohar)
  5. Hammer and tool kit maker
  6. Locksmith
  7. Goldsmith (Sonar)
  8. Potter (Kumhaar)
  9. Sculptor (Moortikar, stone carver) and stone breaker
  10. Cobbler (Charmkar), shoesmith or footwear artisan
  11. Mason (Rajmistri)
  12. Basket, mat or broom maker and coir weaver
  13. Traditional doll and toy maker
  14. Barber (Naai)
  15. Garland maker (Malakaar)
  16. Washerman (Dhobi)
  17. Tailor (Darzi)
  18. Fishing net maker

A lot of One District One Product crafts fall into these trades. Here's how some of the districts we've covered match up:

Who it doesn't cover: handloom weavers (silk saree clusters, for example), painters such as Madhubani artists, and food processors aren't among the 18 trades. If your ODOP business is a saree unit, a pickle brand or a spice processor, this scheme isn't for you. Look at a credit-linked option such as PMEGP instead, and remember that you can't hold both within a 5-year window.

Eligibility: who should apply and who should skip

Apply if you:

  • are 18 or older and work with your hands and tools in one of the 18 trades
  • are self-employed in the unorganised sector
  • haven't taken a loan in the last 5 years under a similar government credit scheme (PMEGP, PM SVANidhi, Mudra). If you've fully repaid a SVANidhi or Mudra loan, you can still apply.
  • are the only person in your family applying. Husband, wife and unmarried children count as one family.

Skip it if you:

  • or anyone in your family is a government employee
  • run a trading or machine-based unit where you don't do the craftwork yourself
  • need ₹10–20 lakh for a factory. The ₹3 lakh cap is for tools and working capital, not for a full plant.

Documents to carry

  • Aadhaar card. Your mobile number must be linked to it, because there's an OTP and a biometric check.
  • Mobile number
  • Bank account details (passbook). If you don't have an account, the CSC can help you open one.
  • Ration card. If you don't have one, bring the Aadhaar cards of all family members.

PM Vishwakarma Yojana online apply via CSC: step-by-step

  1. Confirm the window is open. Call or visit your CSC or DIC first and ask whether new registrations are being taken in your district. You can also call the helpline on 1800 267 7777. As of 2 November 2026 we found no official notice saying registrations are open or closed. The How to Register page still describes the CSC process, and the portal homepage shows an unexplained "Coming shortly" banner.
  2. Go to the CSC in person. The biometric check needs your fingerprint, so nobody else can do it for you.
  3. The operator logs in with their CSC credentials through the CSC login on the portal, then verifies you with a mobile OTP and Aadhaar biometric e-KYC.
  4. Fill in the form: personal details, family details (ration card), your trade, bank details and whether you need a loan. Pick the trade carefully, because it decides your training and toolkit.
  5. Submit and note the application number. Ask the operator for a printout or screenshot.
  6. Stage 1: Gram Panchayat or ULB verification. Your local body confirms that you really practise the trade.
  7. Stage 2: District Implementation Committee checks the application and recommends it.
  8. Stage 3: Screening Committee gives final approval.
  9. Download your certificate and ID card by logging in as a beneficiary on the same portal.
  10. Do the basic training (5–7 days, ₹500/day stipend). Your toolkit e-voucher of up to ₹15,000 is processed as part of this stage.
  11. Apply for the ₹1 lakh loan through the portal. Once you've repaid it on schedule, move on to the ₹2 lakh tranche.

Can I apply online myself, without a CSC?

The official FAQ says you can register on the portal or get help at a CSC, MSME-DFO or DIC. But the How to Register page says applications are sourced through CSCs. In practice, new registrations go through a CSC operator's login. Home pe mobile se "self-apply" karne wale YouTube videos usually skip this detail.

Login and status check

Once you're registered, use the beneficiary login at pmvishwakarma.gov.in/Login. Enter your Aadhaar-linked mobile number, the OTP and the captcha to check your status and download your certificate. There's no separate public status-check page. Official updates are posted on the What's New page.

Loan maths: paise kaise wapas jayenge

A loan at 5% is cheap, but it still has to be repaid. These are our rough equal-EMI estimates. Your bank's actual schedule may differ.

TrancheTenureApprox. EMIApprox. total interest
₹1,00,00018 months~₹5,800/month~₹4,000
₹2,00,00030 months~₹7,100/month~₹13,200

Reality check: if your craft earns a 30% margin, you need about ₹19,000–24,000 of extra monthly sales just to cover the EMI. Take the loan only if you already know what it will buy: a lathe for wooden toys, a polishing machine for stone, a second sewing machine plus fabric stock. Borrowing "for safety" is how artisans get stuck.

The loan gap: the portal's live dashboard on 2 November 2026 showed 6,34,273 loans sanctioned, worth ₹5,447.98 crore, against 30,00,000 registrations. By our calculation, that's roughly 21% of registrants, with an average of about ₹86,000 per loan. Registration does not guarantee a loan. Keep your bank account active, use UPI for your sales and finish your training. All of that makes your file stronger.

PM Vishwakarma Yojana online apply West Bengal: 2026 status

For about two and a half years, the scheme was stuck in West Bengal because the state hadn't set up the committees that verify applications. In February 2025, the government told the Rajya Sabha that more than 8 lakh applications from the state were pending.

That changed on 14 May 2026, when the new state government set up the State Monitoring Committee and the District Implementation Committees. Rollout began after that. According to IANS (22 May 2026), about 7.79 lakh West Bengal artisans had already enrolled. The scheme's third-anniversary event was held in Kolkata on 17 September 2026.

What Bengal artisans should do now:

  1. If you registered in 2023–25, log in at the portal and check your status. Your application was probably waiting at the verification stage.
  2. If it's still stuck, take your application number to your Gram Panchayat or municipality office and to the district DIC. You can also call the West Bengal nodal office on 033-2214 5999 / 033-2280 1601, which is listed on the official Contact Us page.
  3. If you never registered, ask your CSC whether fresh registrations are open in your district before applying.

Is PM Vishwakarma 2.0 launched?

In August 2026, Business Standard reported that a "PM Vishwakarma 2.0" with bigger loan amounts would launch on 17 September 2026. As of 2 November 2026, we couldn't find any official confirmation that it has launched. Until the portal or the MSME Ministry announces it, treat the ₹1 lakh + ₹2 lakh structure as the current rule.

Fake sites, fees and the helpline

  • Use only pmvishwakarma.gov.in. A lookalike site, pmvishwakarmaa.com (with a double "a"), shows up in search results and is not official.
  • Registration is free. Don't pay any agent for a "form" or a "guaranteed loan".
  • Official helpline: 1800 267 7777 or 17923. You can also email champions@gov.in or call 011-23061574. All of these are listed on the Contact Us page, along with nodal officers for each state.

Frequently asked questions

Can I still apply for PM Vishwakarma Yojana in November 2026?

Possibly, but check first. The national target of 30 lakh registrations has been met; the official portal says it was reached in September 2025. As of 2 November 2026 we found no official notice saying registrations are open or closed. Before you go, ask your nearest CSC or District Industries Centre, or call the helpline on 1800 267 7777, whether new applications are being accepted in your district.

Is PM Vishwakarma registration free?

Yes, registration is free. A CSC operator registers you on pmvishwakarma.gov.in using Aadhaar biometric verification and a mobile OTP. Don't pay any agent for a form or a promised loan. Avoid lookalike sites such as pmvishwakarmaa.com (with a double a), which is not the official portal. If anyone asks for money, report it on the official helpline, 1800 267 7777.

How do I get the ₹2 lakh second loan under PM Vishwakarma?

The ₹2 lakh tranche comes after the first ₹1 lakh loan and is repaid over 30 months at 5%. To be considered, repay the first loan on schedule, keep your loan account regular, use digital payments in your business and finish your training. Then apply through the portal or your bank. Only about 21% of registered artisans had any loan sanctioned by 2 November 2026.

Can I apply for PM Vishwakarma if I have a Mudra loan?

Not if you've taken a loan in the last 5 years under a similar government credit scheme such as PMEGP, PM SVANidhi or Mudra. The exception is people who have fully repaid a SVANidhi or Mudra loan; they can apply. Also, only one member per family can benefit, and government employees and their families can't apply.

Is PM Vishwakarma Yojana available in West Bengal now?

Yes, it is rolling out now. The scheme was stuck in West Bengal for about two and a half years, until the state formed its State Monitoring Committee and District Implementation Committees on 14 May 2026. IANS reported on 22 May 2026 that about 7.79 lakh artisans from the state had already enrolled. Earlier applicants should check their status on the portal login or call the state nodal office on 033-2214 5999.

What is the PM Vishwakarma helpline number?

The official PM Vishwakarma helpline is 1800 267 7777, and you can also call 17923. The portal's Contact Us page also lists the email champions@gov.in, the number 011-23061574 and nodal officers for each state. For help with a specific application, your CSC operator or District Industries Centre is often the fastest route.
