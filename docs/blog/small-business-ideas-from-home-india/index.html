18 Small Business Ideas From Home in India: ₹ Setup Costs, Licences and Your District's ODOP Product

18 small business ideas you can run from home in India: pickles, masala, candles, tailoring, saree resale and more. Each one has a rough ₹ setup cost, a margin range, the licence it needs under 2026 rules, and the ODOP product it fits.

November 1, 2026 · 4 min read

The best small business ideas from home in India are the ones that use what your district already grows or makes. That means pickles, masala, papad, festive hampers, candles, agarbatti, tailoring and handloom resale. Most of them can start on ₹10,000–₹2 lakh. Food units need a ₹100-a-year FSSAI basic registration and nothing more until turnover reaches ₹1.5 crore. Yeh page tumhare liye hai if you have a kitchen, a spare room and a local product nobody has packaged properly yet.

Why everyone is searching this right now

Google Trends shows searches for "small business ideas from home" up +90% in 24 hours (WriteOut data, fetched 30 October 2026). Dhanteras falls on Friday, 6 November 2026 and Diwali (Lakshmi Puja) on Sunday, 8 November 2026 (Prokerala). Calendars differ on the dates for Govardhan Puja and Bhai Dooj, which fall somewhere between 9 and 11 November. Wedding and corporate-gifting orders come after that. As of 1 November 2026 Dhanteras is five days away, so if you want festive orders this year, this week is your launch window. Diyas, gift hampers, sweets and home-bakery orders sell fastest.

Licences you need in 2026

  • FSSAI: since 1 April 2026, basic registration covers food businesses with turnover up to ₹1.5 crore, up from ₹12 lakh. The State licence covers ₹1.5 crore to ₹50 crore, and the Central licence covers anything above that (FSSAI order, 13 March 2026). The registration fee is usually quoted as ₹100 a year, but confirm it on the portal. Apply only at foscos.fssai.gov.in (FoSCoS). This is true even if you sell only on Swiggy, Zomato or Instagram.
  • Udyam: registration is free and needs only Aadhaar and PAN, with no document uploads. It never needs renewing, and you can use your home address. Register only at the official site, udyamregistration.gov.in. The MSMED (Amendment) Act, 2026 received Presidential assent on 13 August 2026. It requires TReDS invoice settlement and mediation within 90 days, and it gives Udyam registration permanent legal status. As of 1 November 2026 no start date has been notified, so the Act is not yet in force.
  • GST for online sellers: a Free Press Journal report said a simplified registration for sellers who sell only through e-commerce platforms would begin on 1 November 2026. We have not seen CBIC confirm that it has gone live, so check gst.gov.in before you rely on it.

Paise kaise aayenge: loans and subsidies

  • PM Mudra: Shishu loans go up to ₹50,000, Kishor from ₹50,000 to ₹5 lakh, and Tarun from ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh. Tarun Plus (₹10–20 lakh) is only for people who have fully repaid a Tarun loan, so first-time borrowers can get up to ₹10 lakh. No collateral is needed. Read the scheme details at mudra.org.in, then apply at a bank or online through JanSamarth or Udyamimitra. According to PIB, 57.79 crore Mudra loans worth ₹40.07 lakh crore had been sanctioned as of 27 March 2026. Mudra says it uses no agents, so don't pay anyone to "arrange" a loan.
  • PMFME: this scheme gives food micro-units a 35% credit-linked capital subsidy, up to ₹10 lakh. Projects that match the district's ODOP product get priority, which is why the table above maps every food idea to a district. Apply at pmfme.mofpi.gov.in.
  • PMEGP: a margin-money subsidy of 15–35% on new units, depending on your category and whether you're rural or urban.
  • No PAN or GST? Your bank or MFI can issue a certificate through SIDBI's Udyam Assist Platform (toll-free 180-022-6753).

Who should start, who should skip

Start if you can sell your first 50 units to people you know, and you live near a raw-material source such as chilli mandis, weavers or makhana farms. Skip food ideas if your kitchen can't be kept separate and clean. Skip saree resale if you can't hold ₹1 lakh or more in stock for 3–4 months.

5-step launch roadmap

  1. Pick the idea from the table that matches your district's ODOP product.
  2. Get Udyam registration (free), plus FSSAI if you're selling food.
  3. Make a test batch of ₹10,000–20,000 and sell it on WhatsApp before Dhanteras (6 November 2026) and Diwali (8 November 2026).
  4. Once the orders repeat, apply for a Mudra Shishu or Kishor loan, or PMFME support.
  5. List on marketplaces after checking whether the new GST e-commerce route actually applies to you.

Frequently asked questions

What small business can I start from home with ₹10,000?

With ₹10,000 you can start crochet goods, decorated diyas, Madhubani or folk painting, home tuition or a small tiffin trial. These need little equipment and mostly cost you your time. Make a test batch, sell to people you know on WhatsApp, and put the profit back into raw material before you take any loan.

Do I need an FSSAI licence to sell homemade food?

Yes. Home bakers, pickle makers and tiffin services need FSSAI basic registration, including if they sell through Swiggy, Zomato or Instagram. Since 1 April 2026 it covers turnover up to ₹1.5 crore (it used to be ₹12 lakh). The fee is usually quoted as ₹100 a year, so confirm it on foscos.fssai.gov.in, where you apply. The State licence covers ₹1.5 crore to ₹50 crore.

Is Udyam registration compulsory for a home business?

No. Udyam registration is free, voluntary and self-declared. You need only Aadhaar and PAN, and you can use your home address. It is still worth doing because banks use it for priority-sector loans. The MSMED (Amendment) Act, 2026 gives it permanent legal status, but as of 1 November 2026 no start date for the Act had been notified.

How do I get a Mudra loan for a home business?

Apply at a bank or online through JanSamarth or Udyamimitra. Shishu covers loans up to ₹50,000, Kishor ₹50,000 to ₹5 lakh, and Tarun ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh, all without collateral. Tarun Plus, up to ₹20 lakh, is only for people who have repaid a Tarun loan. Mudra uses no agents, so never pay anyone a fee to get you a loan.

Do I need GST to sell on Amazon, Flipkart or Meesho?

Most marketplaces generally require GST registration. A Free Press Journal report said a simplified GST registration for sellers who sell only through e-commerce platforms would begin on 1 November 2026, with input tax credit capped at ₹2.5 lakh a month. CBIC has not confirmed that it has gone live, so check gst.gov.in before you register.
