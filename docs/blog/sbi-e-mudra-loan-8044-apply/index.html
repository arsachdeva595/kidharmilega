SBI e Mudra Loan 8044: How to Apply Online for Up to ₹50,000, Plus Bank of Baroda and PNB e-Mudra Compared

"8044" isn't a new loan scheme. It's the port number in the address of SBI's e-Mudra portal. This guide gives the correct link, the full application steps, the real ₹50,000 and ₹1 lakh limits, and how SBI compares with Bank of Baroda and PNB.

November 2, 2026 · 7 min read

"8044" in "SBI e Mudra loan 8044" isn't a scheme or a helpline. It's the port number in the address of SBI's e-Mudra portal. The current official link is https://emudra.sbi.bank.in:8044/emudra. If you've had an SBI savings or current account for at least 6 months, you can apply there for up to ₹50,000 online. The e-Mudra maximum is ₹1 lakh, and anything above ₹50,000 needs a branch visit.

Yeh page tumhare liye hai if you're a first-time founder with a small business, or about to start one, and want working capital without running around branches. Below you'll find the step-by-step SBI application, what the old 8044 links mean, how fake loan links misuse the term, and a side-by-side table for SBI, Bank of Baroda and PNB e-Mudra.

TL;DR: SBI e-Mudra in 30 seconds

  • Official portal: emudra.sbi.bank.in:8044/emudra. Check that the address ends in sbi.bank.in before typing anything.
  • Who can apply: micro entrepreneurs whose SBI savings or current account is at least 6 months old.
  • Limit: up to ₹50,000 instantly online (subject to eligibility), and up to ₹1 lakh in total. Tenure goes up to 5 years.
  • You'll need: Aadhaar updated in your SBI account, an Aadhaar-linked mobile for OTPs, and your business name, start date and address.
  • Not an SBI customer? e-Mudra won't work for you. Use JanSamarth or a bank branch.

What is 8044, and why won't the old link open?

A port number tells the browser which "door" of a server to knock on. SBI runs e-Mudra on port 8044, so the number shows up in every version of the address. The domain in front of it has changed over the years:

AddressStatus
emudra.sbi.co.in:8044Old domain, still quoted in many blogs
emudra.bank.sbi:8044Older domain
emudra.sbi.bank.in:8044Current. It loaded when we checked on 2 November 2026

The move to .bank.in follows the RBI requirement for banks to use the .bank.in domain. If a video or WhatsApp forward sends you to the old .co.in address, ignore it and use the current one.

SBI e Mudra loan 8044: eligibility and limits

This is what the official portal says. Many blogs mix it up with the overall Mudra scheme limits.

  • Existing SBI customer only: your savings or current account must be at least 6 months old.
  • Up to ₹50,000: instant online processing, if you meet the eligibility rules.
  • ₹50,000 to ₹1 lakh: you must visit your branch to sign documents. After that you get an SMS, and you have to finish the process on the portal within 30 days of that sanction SMS. Miss the window and you'll probably have to start again.
  • Maximum: ₹1 lakh, repaid over up to 5 years.
  • No Aadhaar in your account? The request is handled manually at the branch, so it's no longer instant.
  • Street vendors: the portal also has an e-Mudra–PM SVANidhi option. It needs your vendor ID or vending certificate and a UPI ID.

Interest rate: the portal doesn't publish one. The rate is linked to SBI's benchmark lending rates. Third-party sites quote anything from about 9.75% to 12.35%, but those figures are unofficial and vary. Ask your branch, or check the sanction terms before you e-sign.

Documents and details to keep ready

  • SBI account number and branch name
  • Business proof: business name, start date and address
  • Aadhaar updated in your SBI account, and the Aadhaar-linked mobile (OTPs for e-KYC and e-Sign go to this number)
  • Category: General, SC, ST, OBC or Minority
  • GSTN, Udyog Aadhaar/Udyam or shop registration, if you have them
  • Scanned uploads in PDF, JPEG or PNG, 2 MB maximum each

How to apply for SBI e-Mudra online: step by step

The screens change slightly from time to time, but the order stays the same:

  1. Open the official portal: go to https://emudra.sbi.bank.in:8044/emudra on a laptop or phone. Type the address yourself. Don't click links from SMS or WhatsApp.
  2. Pick the right product: choose regular e-Mudra, or e-Mudra–PM SVANidhi if you're a registered street vendor.
  3. Read the instructions and continue: the portal lists the eligibility rules and documents first. Check you meet them before going further.
  4. Enter your basic details: mobile number, SBI account number and the loan amount. Keep it at ₹50,000 or less if you want the fully online route.
  5. Fill in your business details: business name, start date, address, category, and your GSTN, Udyam or shop registration number if you have one.
  6. Upload documents: PDF, JPEG or PNG under 2 MB each. Compress phone photos before uploading.
  7. Complete Aadhaar e-KYC: enter the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile.
  8. e-Sign the loan agreement: read the terms (amount, rate, tenure) and approve with another Aadhaar OTP.
  9. Watch for SMS updates: for amounts up to ₹50,000, sanction and credit happen online if you're eligible. For amounts above ₹50,000, visit the branch, then come back to the portal and finish within 30 days of the SMS.

Bank of Baroda e Mudra loan: what's different

The Bank of Baroda Digital Mudra Loan is for individuals and sole proprietors who already run a non-farm business in manufacturing, trading or services. It's collateral-free with CGTMSE cover, and it has nil processing and prepayment charges, which is a real saving. You'll need a Udyam registration and PAN, so register on Udyam first if you haven't. Apply through JanSamarth.

PNB e Mudra loan: proceed carefully

PNB's e-Mudra page (pnb.bank.in/e-Mudra.html) returned a 404 error when we checked on 2 November 2026, so we couldn't confirm the limit, rate or exact apply link from PNB itself. Search snippets of that page say it covers non-farm enterprises and needs an active PNB savings account for the past 12 months. Applications go through PNB's Insta Loans service. Before you apply, call PNB's loan helpline on 1800 180 8888 or visit your branch. Don't trust "PNB e-Mudra apply" links from YouTube descriptions.

Fake loan links that use "8044": how to spot them

On 22 June 2026, PIB Fact Check flagged a fake letter promising a "₹10 lakh Mudra loan" in exchange for a ₹6,550 fee (Outlook Money). The same warning was doing the rounds again in October 2026. Use these checks:

  • Check the domain: the real SBI address ends in sbi.bank.in before ":8044". Lookalikes such as "emudra-sbi.in", "sbiemudra.online" or links through URL shorteners are not SBI.
  • No upfront fee: no bank asks for a "file charge", "insurance" or "approval fee" via UPI before sanctioning a Mudra loan.
  • MUDRA doesn't lend to you directly: according to PIB, MUDRA refinances banks, NBFCs and MFIs. A "MUDRA approval letter" sent to an individual is a red flag.
  • "₹20 lakh e-Mudra" is a mix-up: ₹10–20 lakh is Tarun Plus, in effect since 24 October 2024. It's only for borrowers who have already repaid a Tarun loan. SBI's e-Mudra route tops out at ₹1 lakh.
  • Never share OTPs with an "agent". The only OTPs you enter are for e-KYC and e-Sign, on the official portal, by yourself.

Paise kaise aayenge: is ₹50,000 enough for your business?

For a sense of the EMI, here's ₹50,000 at an assumed 10% a year (an illustration, not SBI's rate). That works out to about ₹1,613 a month over 3 years or ₹1,062 a month over 5 years. If your business can't comfortably make ₹2,000–3,000 extra profit a month, borrowing won't help yet.

₹50,000 works best as working capital for low-setup, district-rooted products such as mushroom growing in Arwal, lac bangles in Supaul or Madhubani paintings, where raw material and a first batch fit inside this amount. You can browse more district-wise ideas on our ODOP business guides homepage.

Who should use SBI e-Mudra

  • You've had an SBI account for 6+ months, your Aadhaar and mobile are linked, and you need ₹50,000 or less fast.
  • You already run a small business, even an unregistered one, and can show a name, address and start date.

Who should skip it

  • You need more than ₹1 lakh. Go to a branch for Kishore (₹50,000–5 lakh) or Tarun (₹5–10 lakh), or apply through JanSamarth.
  • You don't bank with SBI, or your account is newer than 6 months.
  • Your business idea isn't tested yet. Run a small pilot from savings first, then borrow.

Frequently asked questions

What does 8044 mean in SBI e Mudra loan?

8044 is the port number in the web address of SBI's e-Mudra portal. It isn't a separate scheme, loan amount or helpline. The current official address is emudra.sbi.bank.in:8044/emudra. Older addresses like emudra.sbi.co.in:8044 still appear in blogs and videos, but they use outdated domains. Always type the current address yourself.

Can I get ₹50,000 from SBI e-Mudra without visiting the branch?

Yes, if you meet the eligibility rules. You need an SBI savings or current account that's at least 6 months old, with Aadhaar updated and an Aadhaar-linked mobile for OTPs. Loans up to ₹50,000 are processed instantly online. For ₹50,000 to ₹1 lakh you must sign documents at the branch, then complete the portal process within 30 days of the sanction SMS.

Can a non-SBI customer apply for SBI e-Mudra?

No. SBI e-Mudra is only for existing SBI account holders whose account is at least 6 months old. If you bank elsewhere, check your own bank's digital Mudra option. Bank of Baroda, for example, runs its Digital Mudra loan through JanSamarth. You can also apply through the JanSamarth portal or visit a branch for a regular Mudra loan.

Is there a ₹20 lakh e-Mudra loan?

No. ₹20 lakh is the ceiling of the Tarun Plus category under the wider Mudra scheme, in effect since 24 October 2024. It's only for borrowers who have already repaid a Tarun loan. SBI's e-Mudra online route tops out at ₹1 lakh, and only ₹50,000 of that is instant. Headlines promising ₹20 lakh through e-Mudra are misleading.

What is the Bank of Baroda e Mudra loan limit?

Bank of Baroda's Digital Mudra loan disburses up to ₹50,000 digitally, and its official page lists tiers up to ₹10 lakh. It's for individuals and sole proprietors aged 18 to 60 with an existing non-farm business. You need PAN, Udyam, GSTN if registered, and six months of bank statements. Processing and prepayment charges are nil. Apply through JanSamarth.

Is a Mudra loan approval letter asking for a fee genuine?

No. On 22 June 2026, PIB Fact Check called a letter promising a ₹10 lakh Mudra loan for a ₹6,550 fee fake. Banks don't collect approval or file fees over UPI before sanctioning a loan, and MUDRA doesn't lend directly to individuals. Report such messages and apply only through your bank's official portal or branch.
