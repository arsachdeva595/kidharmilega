PMEGP Loan Interest Rate Calculator 2026: Subsidy, EMI, Documents, Apply Online Login and Status

PMEGP has no fixed interest rate. Lender guides quote about 11–12% (unofficial), and a 15–35% margin-money subsidy shrinks the amount you actually repay. This calculator shows your subsidy, your own contribution and your EMI, with a worked Gujarat example, the document list and how to log in and check status on the KVIC portal.

November 2, 2026 · 8 min read

The government doesn't fix a PMEGP interest rate. Each bank prices the loan under its own MSME lending rules. Lender guides put the usual rate at about 11–12% a year, but that figure is unofficial, so check your bank's actual rate. The scheme helps in a different way. It pays a 15–35% margin-money subsidy on your project cost, so you repay a much smaller loan. This PMEGP loan interest rate calculator guide shows the subsidy, your own contribution and your EMI with real ₹ figures. You apply only on the official KVIC PMEGP e-portal.

Yeh page tumhare liye hai if you have ₹50,000 to ₹5 lakh saved and want to know, before you visit a bank, how big your EMI will be and paise kaise aayenge.

TL;DR: PMEGP in 30 seconds

  • Interest rate: set by your bank. Lender guides quote about 11–12% p.a., and some give a wider 8–12% range. These are unofficial figures.
  • Subsidy: 15% (urban) or 25% (rural) for general category. 25% (urban) or 35% (rural) for special category.
  • Own contribution: 10% for general category, 5% for special category.
  • Maximum project cost: ₹50 lakh for manufacturing, ₹20 lakh for service.
  • Last date: none. Applications stay open all year and there's no fee.
  • Apply: New individual application on kviconline.gov.in.

PMEGP loan interest rate: what banks actually charge

PMEGP loans come from public sector banks, private banks, regional rural banks and cooperative banks approved under the scheme. Each one adds its own spread to its benchmark rate. So two founders with the same project in the same district can get different rates. Most lender guides put the figure at 11–12% p.a., though this is not an official number.

What changes your rate:

  • The bank's current MSME lending rate. Ask for the rate in writing at two branches.
  • Your CIBIL score and any existing loans.
  • Project size. Larger loans may need CGTMSE cover or collateral. Ask your branch what applies above ₹10 lakh.

The trick most YouTube videos miss: the subsidy isn't paid to you in cash. The bank keeps it as a 3-year term deposit (TDR) in your name. Once your unit passes physical verification, the money is adjusted against your loan. Under the 2022 guidelines, banks shouldn't charge interest on the part of the loan equal to the subsidy while it sits in that TDR. In practice your interest cost is roughly what you'd pay on loan minus subsidy. Confirm this with your bank and the PMEGP Guidelines 2022 PDF before you sign.

PMEGP loan interest rate calculator: 4 steps

You need five inputs: project cost, category (general or special), area (rural or urban), interest rate and tenure. The guidelines allow repayment over 3–7 years, usually after a moratorium the bank decides.

Step 1: Find your subsidy and own-contribution percentages

CategoryOwn contributionSubsidy (urban)Subsidy (rural)
General10%15%25%
Special (SC/ST/OBC/minority/women/ex-servicemen/transgender/persons with disabilities/NE region/hill/border/aspirational districts)5%25%35%

Step 2: Run the formula

  1. Own contribution = Project cost × 10% (or 5%)
  2. Bank sanction = Project cost − Own contribution
  3. Subsidy (TDR) = Project cost × your subsidy %
  4. Effective loan you repay = Bank sanction − Subsidy
  5. EMI = (Effective loan ÷ ₹1 lakh) × EMI-per-lakh from the table below

PMEGP loan subsidy details: the conditions that matter

  • New units only. An existing business can't take a first PMEGP loan.
  • Age 18+ and no income limit. You need Class VIII pass for projects above ₹10 lakh (manufacturing) or ₹5 lakh (service).
  • EDP training is mandatory. You must finish the Entrepreneurship Development Programme before the subsidy is released.
  • 3-year lock-in. The subsidy stays in the TDR for 3 years and is adjusted against your loan only if the unit is running and passes verification. If you shut down early, you can lose it.
  • Land isn't counted. Budget for land or rent separately from your project cost.
  • Second loan for expansion. Existing PMEGP units can apply for up to ₹1 crore (manufacturing) or ₹25 lakh (service) with 15% subsidy, or 20% in the NE region and hill states. Check these figures in the guidelines PDF. The second-loan login is a separate page.

Scale check: since FY 2014-15, more than 8.38 lakh units have received over ₹25,193 crore in subsidy. About 80% of them are rural and 40% are owned by women.

PMEGP loan scheme in Gujarat: a Junagadh peanut-unit example

Gujarat doesn't have a separate PMEGP portal. You apply on the same national KVIC portal. Rural units are handled by KVIC or the Gujarat KVIB, and urban units by the District Industries Centre (DIC). We found no Gujarat-specific PMEGP notice for 2026.

Take a woman founder from a Junagadh village setting up a peanut-chikki and roasted-peanut unit. Peanuts are the district's ODOP product (see our Junagadh groundnut business guide).

  1. Project cost: ₹15 lakh (roaster, chikki line, packing machine, first working-capital cycle). Land not included.
  2. Category: woman = special, rural, so she qualifies for 35% subsidy = ₹5.25 lakh.
  3. Own contribution: 5% = ₹75,000.
  4. Effective loan: ₹14.25 lakh − ₹5.25 lakh = ₹9 lakh, so the EMI is about ₹19,791 at 11.5% over 5 years.
  5. Breakeven check: she needs roughly ₹20,000 a month of net profit just to cover the EMI. At a 15–20% net margin, that means about ₹1–1.3 lakh a month in sales, before her own salary.

The same maths works for an Amreli peanut-products unit or a Mehsana dairy unit. Only the project cost changes.

PMEGP loan documents required

  • Aadhaar card, linked to an active mobile number (registration uses an OTP)
  • PAN card
  • Detailed project report with costs, machinery quotes and projected sales
  • Passport-size photo
  • Education certificate (Class VIII or above if your project needs it)
  • Caste or special-category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/minority/ex-servicemen/disability)
  • Rural-area certificate if you're claiming the rural subsidy rate
  • EDP training certificate, uploaded after training
  • Bank account details

Get these right first. According to a Lok Sabha answer, about half of applications are rejected at the district task force stage, mostly because documents are incomplete or details are vague.

PMEGP loan apply online login: step-by-step

  1. Open the PMEGP e-portal home.
  2. Click the application form for individuals (direct link). SHGs, societies and trusts use the non-individual form.
  3. Enter your Aadhaar number and verify it with the OTP.
  4. Fill in your personal details, category, unit location (rural or urban), activity, project cost and up to three preferred banks.
  5. Upload your documents and project report, then submit. Note down the Application ID / User ID the portal gives you.
  6. Use "Login for Registered Applicant" on the portal home to edit, upload or track your application.
  7. Go to the district task force interview if called. The file then goes to the bank, you complete EDP, the loan is disbursed and the subsidy is parked in a TDR.

There is no application fee. If an agent asks for money to "get PMEGP approved", walk away.

PMEGP loan status check

Log in through "Login for Registered Applicant" on the portal home with your Application ID or user ID and password. The dashboard shows where your file is: with the agency, the task force, the bank, EDP, or subsidy claim. If the file has sat with the bank for weeks, take your Application ID to the branch manager and the DIC or KVIB office.

PMEGP loan apply online last date: is there one?

No. PMEGP applications are open all year. The last formal approval we could find covered FY 2021-22 to FY 2025-26 (PIB). The scheme is still clearly running in FY 2026-27, because the ministry released subsidy on 28 October 2026 (PIB). Ignore any video that claims a "last date".

What is "PMEGP loan govt guru"?

We found no official PMEGP page by that name. It looks like a YouTube or blog brand, not a government site. Use only kviconline.gov.in, myScheme or the KVIC website, and never share your Aadhaar OTP with anyone.

Who should apply, and who should skip

Apply if: you're starting a new unit, have 5–10% of the project cost in cash, can support yourself for 6–12 months, and have a product with local demand, such as your district's ODOP product.

Skip if: you're expanding a non-PMEGP business (you're not eligible), you need the subsidy money as cash in year one (it's locked for 3 years), or your projected monthly profit doesn't clear the EMI by at least 1.5×.

To find a product to pair with your PMEGP loan, browse the district guides on Kidhar Milega. Each one lists setup costs, margins and breakeven months.

Frequently asked questions

What is the PMEGP loan interest rate in 2026?

The government doesn't fix one. Each bank sets the rate under its MSME lending rules. Lender guides quote about 11–12% a year, and some give a wider 8–12% range, but these are unofficial figures. Your rate depends on the bank, your credit score and the project size, so get written quotes from at least two branches before you choose a bank in your application.

How is PMEGP EMI calculated?

Subtract your own contribution (5% or 10%) from the project cost to get the bank sanction. Subtract the subsidy (15–35%) from that to get the effective loan, then apply a normal EMI formula. For example, a ₹10 lakh rural general-category unit leaves a ₹6.5 lakh effective loan, which works out to about ₹14,295 a month at 11.5% over 5 years.

Is there a last date to apply for PMEGP?

No. PMEGP applications are accepted all year on the KVIC e-portal, with no fee. The last formal scheme approval covered up to FY 2025-26, but the scheme is still running in FY 2026-27. The MSME ministry released ₹1,000 crore of subsidy for 11,333 new micro-enterprises on 28 October 2026.

When do I get the PMEGP subsidy money?

You don't receive it as cash. The bank keeps the subsidy in a 3-year term deposit in your name. After three years, if your unit is running and passes physical verification, it's adjusted against your loan. You must also finish EDP training before the subsidy is released.

How do I check my PMEGP loan status?

Go to the PMEGP e-portal on kviconline.gov.in, click 'Login for Registered Applicant' and sign in with your Application ID or user ID. The dashboard shows whether your file is with the implementing agency, the task force, the bank or the subsidy stage. If it's stuck, visit your bank branch or the DIC or KVIB office with your ID.
