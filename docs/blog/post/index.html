Loan Waiver 2026: Does It Cover PMEGP, Mudra or MSME Business Loans?

The October 2026 loan waiver news is about Maharashtra crop loans. PMEGP, Mudra and CGTMSE business loans are not covered and are not waived. Here's how the PMEGP subsidy actually works, and what to do if you can't keep up with EMIs.

October 31, 2026 · Updated October 31, 2026 · 3 min read

No. The loan waiver in the news covers farm crop loans only. It does not cover PMEGP, Mudra or CGTMSE-backed business loans, and no central or state scheme has waived these loans. If you took a business loan for an ODOP unit, you still have to repay it. What you can do is use the PMEGP subsidy properly and ask your bank to restructure the loan early if EMIs start to hurt. Why "loan waiver" is trending right now

On Google Trends, searches for "loan waiver" in India crossed 2,000+ with a +100% breakout, starting 24 October 2026. The spike comes from Maharashtra's Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar Shetkari Karjmukti Yojana 2026:

  • 27–28 October 2026: the cabinet approved a 4th list of about 4.15 lakh farmers, as reported by Saamana.
  • 29–30 October 2026: a government resolution (GR) released ₹4,000 crore, according to The Hitavada.
  • By 27 October, about ₹13,275 crore had been credited to 15,85,493 farmers, per Deshdoot.

The scheme waives short-term crop loans of up to ₹2 lakh that were disbursed between 1 April 2019 and 31 March 2025 and were overdue on 30 September 2025. A one-time settlement option has been reported for larger dues. Business loans are not part of it. Official details are on the Cooperation Commissioner's website. The finance minister has also said that no full central waiver is under consideration.

PMEGP subsidy is not a waiver

Many founders mix these two up. The PMEGP margin money subsidy works like this:

  • General category: 15% subsidy in urban areas, 25% in rural areas. You put in 10% yourself.
  • Special category (SC/ST/OBC, women, minorities, ex-servicemen, PwD, NER, hill and border areas): 25% subsidy in urban areas, 35% in rural areas. You put in 5% yourself.
  • The subsidy is parked in a term deposit for 3 years. It is adjusted against your loan only if the unit is still running after that lock-in.

Paise kaise aayenge? Here's an example. A rural ₹10 lakh project in the general category gets ₹2.5 lakh of subsidy. You contribute ₹1 lakh and the bank lends ₹9 lakh, but you pay EMIs on the full ₹9 lakh for the first 3 years. If the unit shuts down early, you can lose the subsidy. Apply only through the KVIC PMEGP portal.

EMIs getting hard? Do this, in this order

  1. Act before 30 days overdue. Banks flag an account as SMA-0 at 1–30 days overdue, SMA-1 at 31–60 days and SMA-2 at 61–90 days. After 90 days it becomes an NPA and your CIBIL score drops sharply.
  2. Write to the branch manager. Explain the cash-flow problem with 6 months of bank statements and your GST returns or sales records.
  3. Ask for restructuring. Options include a longer tenure, a short moratorium, or converting part of the overdue amount into a separate term loan.
  4. Use the MSME rehabilitation framework. Under the RBI/MSME Ministry framework for reviving stressed MSMEs, banks have committees for stressed accounts. A Udyam-registered borrower can approach them directly.
  5. Treat one-time settlement as the last option. It closes the loan, but your credit report will show it as "settled". That hurts future PMEGP or Mudra applications.

Who should worry and who can relax

Relax if you're a Maharashtra farmer with an eligible crop loan. Check the list at your bank branch or village society and complete e-KYC at an Aaple Sarkar Seva Kendra.

Plan carefully if you're about to borrow ₹5–20 lakh for a processing unit. Build in a 6-month cash buffer and don't bet on a waiver. Seasonal products like Saharsa makhana, Guntur chillies or Vizianagaram mango jelly have uneven cash flow, so ask the bank for a moratorium that matches your season.

Frequently asked questions

Is the PMEGP loan waived by the government?

No. PMEGP gives a 15–35% margin money subsidy on the project cost, but the bank loan itself has to be repaid in full. The subsidy stays in a term deposit for 3 years and is adjusted against the loan only if the unit is still running after that period. No government has announced a PMEGP loan waiver.

Is there a Mudra loan waiver in 2026?

No. As of 31 October 2026, no central or state government has announced a Mudra loan waiver. Mudra loans don't need collateral because a credit guarantee fund covers the bank, but that guarantee protects the lender, not the borrower. Messages promising a 'Mudra karj mafi' are false and often scams.

What happens if I default on a CGTMSE-backed loan?

The bank can claim part of its loss from the CGTMSE guarantee, but that doesn't cancel your debt. The bank can still recover the remaining dues from you, the account is reported as an NPA, and your credit score drops. That makes future business loans much harder to get.

Which loan waiver is in the news in October 2026?

It's Maharashtra's Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar Shetkari Karjmukti Yojana 2026. It waives short-term crop loans of up to ₹2 lakh. A 4th list of about 4.15 lakh farmers was approved on 27–28 October 2026, and a GR released ₹4,000 crore on 29–30 October 2026. It doesn't cover business loans.

Can I restructure my MSME loan if sales drop?

Yes. Approach your bank before the account is 30 days overdue and ask for a longer tenure or a moratorium. Under the RBI and MSME Ministry framework for reviving stressed MSMEs, banks have committees for stressed accounts, and Udyam-registered borrowers can apply to them directly with their financial records.
