opportunity, Dhanbad mein.

Dhanbad district in Jharkhand contains the Jharia Coalfield, stretching across multiple mine blocks in the heart of eastern India's industrial belt. Proximity to coal reserves eliminates sourcing friction; BCCL and private mining operators work from Dhanbad itself. Buyers span India's eastern industrial corridor (West Bengal, Odisha steel mills) and port cities (Kolkata, Vishakhapatnam) for export.

Step-by-Step Roadmap

Zero se pehli sale tak — actual kaam

Har step realistic hai — Dhanbad ke liye, as is.

1
Learn / Research
Visit Jharia Coalfield mineheads near Belgaum-Dhanbad border; call 5 BCCL contractors for raw material rates and supply terms; attend the bi-monthly coal trade meetups at Dhanbad Chamber of Commerce; register free Udyam profile online — zero cost, doable this week
2
Source Raw Materials
Source coal directly from BCCL (Bharat Coking Coal Limited) coal blocks or licensed contractors in Jharia. First batch: 100-500 MT costs ₹30-50L depending on grade (coking vs thermal). Supplier hub: Belgaum, Jharia, Muraidih blocks near Dhanbad city center.
3
Produce / Partner
Coal trading requires minimal processing if you buy ready-mined coal; partner with BCCL-licensed beneficiation units in Dhanbad (coal washing, sizing, blending) on a margin-share model, or rent a 2000-3000 sqm coal washing unit for ₹3-8L/month. Start with broker/trader model (zero processing) before upgrading to beneficiation.
4
Brand & Register
Register Udyam (free, 10 mins online). IEC from DGFT (dgft.gov.in); the application fee is ₹500. Obtain coal trader license from Odisha/Jharkhand coal regulatory board (₹10000-30000, 4-6 weeks). File GST (free, 1 week). No An individual does not buy a GI tag from the Ministry of Textiles. A new Geographical Indication is filed by an association of producers with the Geographical Indications Registry (IP India). Once a GI exists, a producer applies as an authorised user on Form GI-3A. The official fee is ₹10.
5
First Sale
First sale: Cold-call thermal power plant procurement heads (lists available via NTPC, DVC websites) or post on IndiaTradeSupport.com. Minimum order: 50 MT. Expected first-month revenue: ₹15-25L (at ₹3000-5000/MT wholesale rate). Payment terms: 15-30 days post-delivery.
6
Scale
At ₹10L/month revenue, hire 1 sales executive + 1 logistics coordinator (₹40000/month combined). PMEGP (December 2023 guidelines, approved through 2025-26): maximum project cost ₹50 lakh for manufacturing and ₹20 lakh for service; margin-money subsidy 15% urban / 25% rural for the general category and 25% urban / 35% rural for special categories; own contribution 10% general and 5% special; for MSE loans sanctioned or renewed on or after 1 April 2026, including PMEGP units, banks must lend without collateral up to ₹20 lakh under RBI's Lending to MSME Sector (Amendment) Directions, 2026 (RBI/2025-26/206, 9 February 2026). Banks may go up to ₹25 lakh without collateral based on track record. Apply at pmegp.msme.gov.in. Connect with port export terminals (Kolkata, Vishakhapatnam) for containerized export. Attend Vibrant Gujarat or Energy Conclave expos to pitch to mills and power plants directly.

Government Schemes

Woh paisa jo tujhe pata nahi tha

🏛
PMEGP (Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme)
New unit under the December 2023 guidelines: maximum project cost ₹50 lakh for manufacturing and ₹20 lakh for service or business. Margin-money subsidy is 15% of project cost in urban areas and 25% in rural areas for the general category, and 25% urban / 35% rural for special categories (SC, ST, OBC, minorities, women, ex-servicemen, transgender persons, persons with disability, NER, hill and border areas, and aspirational districts). Own contribution is 10% for general category and 5% for special categories. For MSE loans sanctioned or renewed on or after 1 April 2026, including PMEGP units, banks must lend without collateral up to ₹20 lakh under RBI's Lending to MSME Sector (Amendment) Directions, 2026 (RBI/2025-26/206, 9 February 2026). Banks may go up to ₹25 lakh without collateral based on the borrower's track record and good repayment, with CGTMSE cover. Apply at pmegp.msme.gov.in (PMEGP 2.0). The scheme was approved through 2025-26; treat any extension as unconfirmed until the Ministry of MSME publishes it.
🏛
PM Mudra Yojana (PMMY)
PMMY ceilings: Shishu up to ₹50,000; Kishore above ₹50,000 and up to ₹5 lakh; Tarun above ₹5 lakh and up to ₹10 lakh; Tarun Plus above ₹10 lakh and up to ₹20 lakh, and only after an earlier Tarun loan has been repaid. The maximum is ₹20 lakh. The lending bank sets the interest rate; the scheme does not fix one rate. From 1 April 2026, MSE loans are collateral-free up to ₹20 lakh under RBI's Lending to MSME Sector (Amendment) Directions, 2026 (RBI/2025-26/206, 9 February 2026); banks may go up to ₹25 lakh based on track record. The Tarun ceiling of ₹10 lakh is the product tier, not that collateral-free limit. Apply through a bank, NBFC or MFI, or at udyamimitra.in.
🏚
Jharkhand Investment Promotion Board
3-5% stamp duty exemption on commercial property leasing for mining-linked businesses
🏚
Jharkhand Mines and Minerals Policy 2020
priority allocation for coal processor licenses and government contract access
Pehla step: udyamregistration.gov.in — free, 10 minute. Udyam registration most schemes ke liye zaroori hai.
