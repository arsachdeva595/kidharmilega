Sesame and Jaggery Based Products ki
opportunity, Gaya mein.

Gaya district sits in the Magadh region of south Bihar. Tilkut is made in Gaya town — Ramna Road is the stretch buyers walk in winter — and in Tekari. Sesame and jaggery are the two inputs. Sesame for this trade has long come through the Gaya mandi; the district has also started sowing til, so a local crop is beginning to sit beside that haul. Bodh Gaya and the Vishnupad temple keep a stream of visitors who already associate the district with this sweet.

Step-by-Step Roadmap

Zero se pehli sale tak — actual kaam

Har step realistic hai — Gaya ke liye, as is.

1
Learn / Research
Go to Gaya in the weeks before Makar Sankranti and walk the tilkut shops on Ramna Road. Watch a batch: sesame is roasted, pounded, and folded into hot jaggery or sugar, then pressed into discs. Ask what the kitchen does after the festival. Many switch to anarsa and laai. This step costs the fare and the sweets you buy to taste. No roaster yet.
2
Source Raw Materials
Buy white sesame and jaggery, the two inputs. The Gaya mandi is the local collection point. A lot of the sesame has been brought in from other states, and some of it is now grown in the district. Taste the jaggery before you take a sack. A dark, harsh gur shows up in the disc. Start with one small lot, enough for a few dozen pieces, not a whole season's stock.
3
Produce / Partner
Roast, pound and set. The versions shops already sell are sugar, jaggery and khowa, with a black-sesame laddu beside them. Work in a room you can clean, with a grinder you can take apart. If you have never held a hot jaggery pan, spend the first season next to a working halwai in Gaya rather than fitting out a shed.
4
Brand & Register
Register Udyam at udyamregistration.gov.in. It is free. Take an FSSAI registration before you sell a pack. This is food. Do not put a GI mark on the label. Application 987 for Gaya Tilkut is under examination and has no certificate. A brand name and the town name are enough.
5
First Sale
The first sale is local and dated. Put boxes out before Tila Sankranti, not in June. Offer the jaggery version and the sugar version side by side and see which one moves. Sweet shops in Patna and the Magadh towns are the wholesale door. One shop that reorders is a better signal than a website.
6
Scale
Wait until one full Sankranti is done. Then add a second outlet, or a packed box for shops that already claim to sell Gaya tilkut. Keep the winter calendar. Use the rest of the year for anarsa, laai or plain sesame packing so the rent is not sitting on one festival. Export is a later conversation. The product is not GI-registered, and the demand you can see is inside India.

PMFME
Pradhan Mantri Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises. The ministry's district list records sesame-based products as Gaya's food ODOP. Read the current subsidy on the MoFPI portal before you put a number in a plan.
🏛
FSSAI registration
required before a packed sweet is sold. Check the current fee on the FSSAI portal rather than copying an old figure.

District Industries Centre, Gaya
ask what the district is actually funding for sesame products this year. The district page is gaya.bihar.gov.in/one-district-one-product/.

Marketing

Ye marketing ideas try karna mat bhulna

  • Short clips in December: the roast, the pound, the disc setting. Say Gaya, say jaggery or sugar, say the date of Sankranti. No GI claim in the caption.
  • Message the sweet shops you already buy from in Gaya and Patna. Send a photo of today's batch and how many discs you have. Talk after they have tasted one, not before.
  • One table for a weekend on the winter shopping stretch, or at the mela. Sell what you made that week. If it sits, change the jaggery ratio before you print boxes.
