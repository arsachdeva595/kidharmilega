Market reality 2026: why now, or why not now?

Tailwinds

  • Bags, wallets and travel accessories have no size issues, so returns are low.
  • Travel is booming, driving demand for luggage and organisers.
  • Kolkata, Kanpur, Mumbai and Delhi have skilled leather and bag workshops.
  • Buyers want vegan leather, sustainable and handcrafted options.

Headwinds and traps

  • Cheap imports dominate marketplaces.
  • Big brands like Caprese, Lavie and American Tourister are strong.
  • Designs are easily copied.
  • Leather prices vary.

What changed in 2026

Travel and work have made bags an everyday purchase, and buyers want better design than cheap imports offer. In 2026, bag and accessory brands grow by owning a need, such as travel organisation, laptop bags or handcrafted leather, and building a recognisable design.

Packing cubes, wardrobe organisers and toiletry bags sell well online, especially before holidays and weddings. Bundles raise order value. Good design and fabric quality set you apart from cheap imports.

The takeaway for a 2026 bootstrapper

Start with 5–8 designs, produce small batches, and reorder what sells.

The cluster advantage: local sourcing and ODOP playbook

No ODOP district specialises in this product, so you will source from wholesale markets near you.

Kolkata (leather goods), Kanpur and Agra (leather), Mumbai's Dharavi (bags) and Delhi make small batches. Working directly with workshops cuts costs.

Licences you'll need

  • GST registration
  • Udyam registration
  • Trademark application
  • Correct labelling of material and maker
  • Export registration if selling abroad

Government support worth checking

  • Udyam registration: Free, online, Aadhaar-based MSME registration. Most MSME benefits, including priority-sector bank loans, start here.
  • PM Mudra Yojana: Collateral-free bank loans in four tiers: Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishore (up to ₹5 lakh), Tarun (up to ₹10 lakh) and Tarun Plus (up to ₹20 lakh, for borrowers who repaid a Tarun loan).
  • PMEGP: Credit-linked subsidy for new units: projects up to ₹50 lakh (manufacturing) or ₹20 lakh (services), with 15–35% of project cost as subsidy depending on your category and whether you are in a rural area.
  • PM Vishwakarma: For registered traditional artisans (potters, toy makers, tailors and other listed trades): skill training with a stipend, a ₹15,000 toolkit incentive and collateral-free loans at 5% interest.

Scheme limits change. Confirm current terms on the official portal or with your bank before applying.

The hard truths: why most attempts fail in year one

  • Copies

    Designs are easily copied. Build a brand, not just a product.

  • Quality

    Stitching and zips fail. Inspect every batch.

  • Cheap imports

    Compete on design and quality, not price.

  • Stock

    Colours and styles that don't sell tie up cash.

  3. Days 46–75

    Sell

    • Launch online.
    • Send to creators.
    • Collect feedback.

  4. Days 76–90

    Review

    • Check margins.
    • Reorder best sellers.
    • Plan new designs.
