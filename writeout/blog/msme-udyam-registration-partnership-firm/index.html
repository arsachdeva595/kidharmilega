MSME Registration for Partnership Firm: Whose Aadhaar, Firm PAN, GSTIN and Udyam Steps (2026)

A partnership firm gets its MSME (Udyam) registration using the managing partner's Aadhaar and the firm's own PAN. It's free, there's nothing to upload, and the certificate usually comes the same day. This guide covers the documents, the steps, LLP and unregistered firms, and the errors that block it.

November 7, 2026 · 8 min read

A partnership firm can do its MSME registration free of cost on the official Udyam portal at udyamregistration.gov.in/UdyamRegistration.aspx. You enter the managing partner's Aadhaar and the firm's own PAN (not a partner's personal PAN). A GSTIN is needed only if the firm is legally required to register under GST.

Nothing is uploaded. It's a self-declaration, and in most cases the Udyam certificate comes through the same day. Below you'll find what to keep ready, the exact steps, and the mistakes that stop the certificate from being issued.

Key facts

  • Official page: New Udyam registration. To print, update or cancel, use the Udyam login page.
  • Fee: ₹0. Sites charging ₹1,500–₹5,000 for "MSME registration" are private agents, not the government.
  • Aadhaar: the managing partner's, verified by OTP.
  • PAN: the partnership firm's PAN.
  • Validity: permanent, with no renewal. Investment and turnover get updated from ITR and GST data.
  • Micro limits (since 1 April 2025): investment up to ₹2.5 crore and turnover up to ₹10 crore.
  • New law: most sections of the MSMED (Amendment) Act, 2026 came into force on 15 October 2026. Under it, registration stays voluntary.

Is MSME registration compulsory for a partnership firm?

No, it isn't compulsory. Under the new section 8 brought in by the MSMED (Amendment) Act, 2026, registration is voluntary for every MSME and is done on a national digital platform that the Centre notifies. In practice, though, you'll need the certificate soon after you start:

  • Buyers ask for it. Larger buyers have to track and report what they owe to registered micro and small suppliers. The amended Act sets a warning first, then ₹10,000–50,000, then ₹50,000–1 lakh as penalties for buyers who don't report.
  • Payment safety. Under the income-tax rule on late payments to MSMEs (section 43B(h) in the old Income-tax Act), a buyer who pays a registered micro or small supplier after 45 days (or 15 days if there's no written agreement) loses the tax deduction for that amount. That pushes buyers to pay on time.
  • PSU and TReDS orders. New section 15A requires central PSUs to settle MSME invoices through TReDS.
  • Loans and schemes. Banks, state subsidy schemes and ODOP support all start by asking for your Udyam number.

Yeh page tumhare liye hai if you and one or more partners are starting a manufacturing, service or trading unit and want the paperwork done right the first time. If you're a sole proprietor, you register with your own Aadhaar and PAN, so the partnership rules here don't apply to you.

Udyam registration for partnership firm: whose Aadhaar, which PAN, GSTIN or not

1. Aadhaar: the managing partner's

The official form says it plainly: for a partnership firm, enter the managing partner's Aadhaar. The OTP goes to the mobile number linked to that Aadhaar, so that partner has to be available while you fill the form.

What if the deed names no managing partner? The portal doesn't give an official answer for this. The safe approach is to have all partners sign a short authorisation (or add a clause to the deed) naming one partner as managing partner, then use that person's Aadhaar. If you're unsure, check with the portal's helpdesk before submitting. Don't just pick whoever's phone is closest.

2. PAN: the firm's, never a partner's

The partnership firm has its own PAN. The 4th character of a firm's PAN is "F", while a personal PAN has "P". Using a partner's personal PAN is the most common mistake: it registers the wrong entity and shows a proprietorship instead of a firm. The portal checks the PAN against Income Tax records, so the firm name has to match the PAN exactly.

3. GSTIN: only if GST registration is mandatory for you

Under notification S.O. 1055(E) dated 5 March 2021, a GSTIN is required only where the firm has to register under the CGST Act, 2017. Small service firms or exempt-goods units below the GST threshold can answer "No" and still register. If you already have a GSTIN, enter it, because turnover data is pulled from it.

Documents to keep ready (nothing gets uploaded)

  • Managing partner's Aadhaar number and the mobile linked to it
  • Firm's PAN card
  • Firm's GSTIN (if applicable)
  • Partnership deed: you won't submit it, but you'll need the date the firm started and the exact firm name
  • Firm's current account number and IFSC
  • Office address and the address of each plant or unit
  • NIC code(s) for your main activity
  • Number of employees (male/female/other) and a rough figure for investment in plant & machinery or equipment

Step-by-step MSME registration for partnership firm

  1. Open the official page. Go to udyamregistration.gov.in/UdyamRegistration.aspx and choose the option for new entrepreneurs who aren't registered yet. If the firm holds an old Udyog Aadhaar (UAM), use the UAM migration page instead.
  2. Aadhaar verification. Enter the managing partner's Aadhaar number and name exactly as printed, give consent, and validate with the OTP.
  3. Type of organisation and PAN. Select Partnership, enter the firm's PAN, and validate. The portal fetches the PAN details and asks whether ITR and GSTIN exist.
  4. Basic details. Mobile, email, the managing partner's social category and gender, and the enterprise name as on PAN.
  5. Official address and plant/unit details. Add each plant or unit separately. Use the full PIN code and district, because district mapping matters for ODOP and state schemes.
  6. Other details. Date of incorporation (when the firm was formed under the deed), date business started, the firm's bank account, the main activity (manufacturing, services or trading), NIC codes and employee count. Choose the main activity carefully, because it drives scheme eligibility.
  7. Investment and turnover. For firms with ITR/GST history, these are filled in from government data. For a new firm, give an honest self-declared figure.
  8. Optional sign-ups. You can tick interest in GeM, TReDS and other government platforms.
  9. Final OTP and submit. You'll get a Udyam Registration Number (format UDYAM-XX-00-0000000). The certificate, with a QR code, can be downloaded from the Udyam login page using the Udyam number and the registered mobile.

One firm gets one Udyam registration, even if it runs several activities or units. Add those inside the same registration instead of creating a second one.

Micro, small or medium? Limits for partnership firms

A firm has to stay within both limits for a category. Crossing either one moves it up a category.

CategoryInvestment up toTurnover up to
Micro₹2.5 crore₹10 crore
Small₹25 crore₹100 crore
Medium₹125 crore₹500 crore

These limits have applied since 1 April 2025. Under the 2026 amendment, investment-plus-turnover is now written into the law as the basis for classification, but future limits will come by notification. A founder pair putting ₹5–20 lakh into machinery will be "Micro" for years.

Registered vs unregistered partnership firm, and where LLPs fit

Unregistered firm (no Registrar of Firms certificate)

The Udyam form asks for the firm's PAN and doesn't ask for a Registrar of Firms registration. A firm with a signed (ideally notarised) deed and its own PAN can therefore usually register. The catch has nothing to do with Udyam: under section 69 of the Indian Partnership Act, 1932, an unregistered firm can't file a civil suit to enforce its contracts. If you're relying on recovering delayed payments, getting the firm registered with your state's Registrar of Firms is cheap insurance.

LLP

An LLP is not a partnership firm on the Udyam form. It's a separate organisation type. Use an authorised signatory's (designated partner's) Aadhaar and the LLP's PAN. The rest of the process is the same.

Partner joins or leaves: updating Udyam

There's no detailed official procedure for a change of partners. Log in at the Udyam login page and choose "Update Details" to edit editable fields such as address, bank, NIC codes and units. Two practical points:

  • If the managing partner changes, the Aadhaar on record no longer matches. Read the portal's circulars or ask the helpdesk whether you need to update or cancel and register again.
  • If the firm is reconstituted with a new PAN, it's legally a new entity, so cancel the old Udyam and register fresh.
  • Use a firm-owned mobile number for the registration, so login OTPs don't leave with an outgoing partner.

Common errors that block the Udyam certificate

  1. Partner's PAN instead of the firm's PAN. This creates the wrong entity type.
  2. Name mismatch. The Aadhaar name or the firm name doesn't match Aadhaar or PAN records character for character.
  3. Aadhaar not linked to an active mobile. No OTP means no registration. Update it at an Aadhaar centre first.
  4. Choosing "Proprietary" or "LLP" by mistake. The organisation type must be Partnership.
  5. Duplicate registration. The firm PAN already holds a Udyam or UAM. Migrate or update instead of registering again.
  6. Wrong NIC code. This doesn't block the certificate, but it can make you ineligible for a manufacturing subsidy later. Fix it through Update Details.
  7. Using a look-alike site. Only udyamregistration.gov.in is official. Sites with names like "udyamregistrar" charge fees for something that's free.

What changed on 15 October 2026

The MSMED (Amendment) Act, 2026 (Act No. 16 of 2026) received assent on 13 August 2026. According to notification S.O. 5575(E) dated 12 October 2026, as reported by TaxGuru and IBClaw, sections 2–7, 11 and 13–17 came into force on 15 October 2026, and section 12 (Facilitation Council makeup) starts on 15 December 2026. The faster dispute timelines (mediation in 90 days, award in 90 days) are in sections 8–10, which have no start date yet. The Act text refers to a "national digital platform" and doesn't name Udyam, but Udyam remains the working portal. For an official overview, see the PIB factsheet and the PRS bill summary.

Lots of firms are registering. A Tripura Star News report on a Ministry workshop says Udyam plus Udyam Assist registrations crossed 10 crore on 30 October 2026. That figure hasn't yet been confirmed in a PIB release.

After the certificate: paise kaise aayenge

Udyam is step one, not the business. Once your firm has its number, the next questions are which product, what setup cost and when you break even. If your partnership has district roots, start with the product your district is already known for, like Guntur chillies, Saharsa makhana or Jamnagar bandhani. Frequently asked questions

Can a partnership firm get Udyam registration?

Yes. A partnership firm registers on udyamregistration.gov.in by selecting 'Partnership' as the organisation type, verifying the managing partner's Aadhaar by OTP and entering the firm's own PAN. It's free, nothing is uploaded, and the certificate is usually generated the same day. Registration is voluntary, but buyers, banks and government schemes routinely ask for it.

Which partner's Aadhaar is used for MSME registration of a partnership firm?

The managing partner's Aadhaar is used, and the official Udyam form says so directly. The OTP goes to the mobile linked to that Aadhaar. If your deed doesn't name a managing partner, have all partners authorise one partner in writing and use that person's Aadhaar. When in doubt, check with the portal helpdesk before you submit.

Is GSTIN mandatory for Udyam registration of a partnership firm?

No. A GSTIN is required only if the firm is legally required to register under the CGST Act, 2017, as set out in notification S.O. 1055(E) of 5 March 2021. A firm below the GST threshold or dealing only in exempt goods can register on Udyam without a GSTIN and add it later through the Update Details option.

Can an unregistered partnership firm apply for Udyam?

Usually yes, because the Udyam form asks for the firm's PAN, not a Registrar of Firms certificate. A firm with a signed deed and its own PAN can register. Keep in mind that under section 69 of the Partnership Act an unregistered firm can't sue to enforce contracts, so registering with the state Registrar of Firms is still a good idea.

Does Udyam registration need renewal every year?

No. Udyam registration is permanent and has no renewal fee or expiry. The portal updates investment and turnover from your ITR and GST data, and your category can change if you cross the limits. It's sensible to log in once a year to check details like address, bank account and NIC codes.

How is an LLP different from a partnership firm on Udyam?

An LLP isn't registered as a partnership firm on the Udyam form; it has its own organisation type. You use a designated partner or authorised signatory's Aadhaar and the LLP's PAN. The steps, fee (free) and classification limits are the same as for any other enterprise.
