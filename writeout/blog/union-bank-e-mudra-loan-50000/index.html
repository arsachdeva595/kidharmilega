Union Bank e Mudra Loan 50,000: How to Apply Online for the Shishu Mudra Loan (Eligibility, Documents, EMI)

Union Bank's "e-Mudra" loan is officially its Shishu Mudra Straight Through Processing loan. It lends up to ₹50,000 without collateral, you apply online, and only existing Union Bank customers can use it. This guide gives the exact apply link, the documents you need, example EMIs at today's 8.25% EBLR, and how to spot a fake approval letter.

November 2, 2026 · 10 min read

Short answer: yes, the Union Bank e Mudra loan of ₹50,000 exists, but Union Bank doesn't call it "e-Mudra". Its official name is Straight Through Processing (Shishu Mudra) for Existing Customers – Upto Rs 50,000. It's a business loan of up to ₹50,000 with no collateral. Existing Union Bank account holders apply online at msme.unionbankofindia.bank.in/#/mudra-main. The loan comes under the government's Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (official site: mudra.org.in). If you don't bank with Union Bank yet, you can't use this online route. You'd apply at a branch, on JanSamarth or on Udyamimitra instead. All three are explained below.

Yeh page tumhare liye hai if you have a small business idea, maybe a craft or food product from your own district, and you want your first ₹50,000 without pledging gold or finding a guarantor. Below you'll find who qualifies, which documents you need, the online steps, what the October 2026 rate hike does to your EMI, and what ₹50k can realistically pay for.

TL;DR

  • Loan: Shishu Mudra, up to ₹50,000, for an income-generating business (term loan, working capital or both).
  • Who: only existing Union Bank customers can use the online STP route.
  • Collateral / guarantor: none. Union Bank's Mudra policy document sets a minimum 5% margin (your own contribution) for Shishu loans, so ask the branch how it applies to you.
  • Tenure: the official pages don't state one. Loan comparison websites say up to 3 years, so confirm with the bank.
  • Rate: Union Bank links it to its repo-linked lending rate (EBLR), which has been 8.25% since 8 October 2026, plus a spread. The bank doesn't publish the Mudra spread, so check with the branch.
  • Action: Apply on Union Bank's MSME portal. Helpline: 1800 2333.

What exactly is the Union Bank Shishu Mudra loan?

Shishu is the smallest of the four categories under the Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY). Union Bank's "Straight Through Processing" (STP) version is processed automatically online. The bank already has your KYC and account history, so it needs less paperwork to assess you. Union Bank's Mudra STP page lists its online routes for Shishu, Kishore, Tarun and Tarun Plus. The Union MUDRA scheme page covers the full scheme.

Who qualifies for the shishu mudra loan from Union Bank of India, and who should skip it

Right for you if:

  • You already have a Union Bank savings or current account with a reasonable transaction history. Comparison websites mention at least 6 months, but Union Bank hasn't confirmed this.
  • You're running, or about to start, a small income-generating business: a craft, a food product, a small shop, tailoring or a service.
  • Your business needs ₹50,000 or less, mostly for raw material, small tools or stock.
  • You can show, or at least plan for, a monthly surplus of around ₹2,000–5,000 to cover the EMI.

Skip it (or pick another route) if:

  • You want a personal loan. Mudra money is only for business activity. It can't pay for weddings, phones or old debts.
  • Your project needs ₹2–5 lakh. Look at a Kishore loan (₹50,001–₹5 lakh) instead of stretching ₹50k.
  • You're not a Union Bank customer. The STP route won't work for you. Apply through a branch, JanSamarth or Udyamimitra.
  • You have unpaid loans or recent bounced EMIs. Clean up your repayment record first, or an online rejection will just waste your time.

Documents for the Union Bank Shishu Mudra loan

Union Bank's official pages don't publish a document list. This list comes from loan comparison websites and the usual STP process, so confirm it with your branch.

  • Aadhaar linked to your mobile number. KYC is done by OTP.
  • PAN card
  • Udyam registration certificate. It's free on the government's Udyam portal and takes about 15 minutes if your Aadhaar and PAN are linked.
  • Bank statements. As an existing customer, your Union Bank statement is usually what the bank looks at.
  • Basic business details: what the business does, its address, expected monthly sales, and what you'll spend the ₹50,000 on.

Tip: write down your spending plan before you start the form, for example "₹28,000 raw lac + ₹12,000 moulds + ₹10,000 packaging". A clear purpose makes the form quicker to fill and holds up better if the branch calls you.

How to apply for the Union Bank e Mudra loan 50,000 online: step by step

We couldn't load every screen of the portal ourselves, so the labels you see may differ slightly. These steps follow the bank's product page and the usual STP process.

  1. Open the official product page: Shishu Mudra STP on unionbankofindia.bank.in.
  2. Click "Apply Now". This takes you to the MSME portal: https://msme.unionbankofindia.bank.in/#/mudra-main.
  3. Verify your mobile number with an OTP. Use the number linked to your Union Bank account.
  4. Complete Aadhaar e-KYC with the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile, then enter your PAN.
  5. Fill in your business details: Udyam number, type of business, location, the loan amount (up to ₹50,000) and what it's for.
  6. Wait for the bank's check. The system checks your account and credit record, then shows whether you're eligible and for how much.
  7. Accept the offer and e-sign the loan documents with an Aadhaar OTP. Check the rate, tenure, EMI and charges before you sign.
  8. Receive the money. If your loan is approved, the money goes into your Union Bank account. Comparison websites put the whole process at about 7–10 days (unverified). Save your application or reference number.

If the portal shows errors, fill in Union Bank's lead form to ask for a callback from a branch, or take the same documents to your home branch.

Not a Union Bank customer? Three other routes

  • Branch application: open an account and apply for a Shishu loan at your nearest Union Bank branch. It takes longer, but first-time borrowers and new businesses can apply this way.
  • JanSamarth: on the government's common portal, jansamarth.in, you can apply for a Mudra loan and choose from participating banks.
  • Udyamimitra: PIB names udyamimitra.in as the proper online channel for Mudra applications.

Remember that MUDRA itself doesn't lend to individuals and doesn't appoint agents. It only refinances banks, NBFCs and MFIs, and those lenders give you the loan (see mudra.org.in). Anyone who tells you "MUDRA ne aapka loan approve kiya hai" is lying.

Union Bank Mudra loan interest rate and EMI after the October 2026 repo hike

Most blogs are out of date on this point. Union Bank's Shishu loan follows its current interest rate circular, linked to the External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR), which moves with RBI's repo rate. Union Bank's interest rates page puts the EBLR at 8.25% from 8 October 2026. Its retail rate sheet breaks this down as the 5.50% repo rate plus a 2.75% spread. Your Mudra rate is the EBLR plus a further spread that the bank doesn't publish. Comparison websites quote rates ranging from 8.75% to "EBLR + 1.25%", and most of those figures are old. So we won't print a fixed rate. Ask your branch or call 1800 2333.

For reference, Union Bank's other benchmarks from 21 October 2026 are: 1-year MCLR 8.85%, overnight MCLR 7.90%, 3-year MCLR 9.05%, and base rate 10.60% (from 1 July 2026).

Paise kaise aayenge? The daily-surplus test

An EMI of ₹1,613 works out to about ₹54 a day. If your business can't reliably leave you ₹60–80 a day after paying for materials and your own time, choose the longest tenure the bank offers, or borrow ₹30,000 instead of ₹50,000.

Fake Mudra approval letters: red flags

PIB Fact Check has repeatedly flagged fake "Mudra approval letters" that ask for upfront fees of ₹350, ₹860, ₹6,550 or ₹7,699 for loans of ₹3–10 lakh. It issued alerts on 21 June and 12 July 2026, and the letters were still circulating in September 2026 (All India Radio News, Business Standard). PIB has also flagged a fake website, onlinemudraindia.co.in, posing as a Mudra lender. Fake "Instant Mudra Loan" apps are circulating too. In a separate case in April 2026, a Union Bank branch manager in Delhi was arrested over 100+ alleged fake Mudra loans (The420). Walk away if you see any of these:

  • A "processing fee", "insurance" or "file charge" to be paid by UPI to a personal account before the loan is sanctioned. Genuine Mudra loans have no approval fee.
  • A letter on "MUDRA" letterhead. MUDRA doesn't lend directly.
  • An "approval" sent over WhatsApp or social media. PIB says MUDRA never sends approvals this way.
  • Links that don't end in .bank.in (Union Bank) or point to jansamarth.in, udyamimitra.in or mudra.org.in.
  • "Agents" promising guaranteed approval. MUDRA doesn't appoint agents, and Union Bank shows its charges inside the official loan documents.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Union Bank e Mudra loan of ₹50,000 real?

Yes. Union Bank calls it 'Straight Through Processing (Shishu Mudra) for Existing Customers – Upto Rs 50,000'. 'e-Mudra' is a name used by YouTube videos and blogs. Existing Union Bank customers can apply online at msme.unionbankofindia.bank.in. The loan needs no collateral, can only be used for a business, and is capped at ₹50,000.

Can I get the Union Bank Shishu Mudra loan without an account in Union Bank?

Not through the online STP route, which only existing Union Bank customers can use. You can still open an account and apply for a Shishu Mudra loan at a Union Bank branch. You can also apply through the government's JanSamarth portal (jansamarth.in) or Udyamimitra (udyamimitra.in) and choose from participating banks. New businesses and first-time borrowers can use these routes.

What is the interest rate on Union Bank Shishu Mudra loan?

It follows Union Bank's current rate circular and is linked to its repo-linked lending rate (EBLR), which has been 8.25% since 8 October 2026, plus a spread. The bank doesn't publish the Mudra spread, and comparison websites quote conflicting, mostly old figures. Ask your branch or call 1800 2333 for the exact rate before you sign.

Do I need a guarantor or collateral for a ₹50,000 Mudra loan?

No. Union Bank says no collateral security is needed for a Mudra loan, and Shishu loans need no guarantor. Union Bank's Mudra policy document does set a minimum 5% margin, your own contribution, for Shishu loans, so ask the branch how it applies to you. You'll also need Aadhaar e-KYC, a PAN and a clean repayment record.

Someone sent me a Mudra approval letter asking for a fee. Is it genuine?

Almost certainly not. PIB Fact Check has repeatedly flagged fake Mudra approval letters asking for fees of ₹350 to ₹7,699, including alerts on 21 June and 12 July 2026. MUDRA does not lend to individuals, appoint agents or send approvals on WhatsApp, and genuine Mudra loans have no approval fee. Apply only through unionbankofindia.bank.in, jansamarth.in, udyamimitra.in or your branch.

What is the tenure and EMI for a ₹50,000 Shishu loan from Union Bank?

Union Bank's official pages don't state a tenure. Loan comparison websites say up to 36 months with monthly EMIs, so confirm with the bank. As an example only, at an assumed 10% rate the EMI on ₹50,000 is about ₹1,613 over 36 months or ₹4,396 over 12 months. Your actual EMI depends on the bank's current EBLR-linked rate.
