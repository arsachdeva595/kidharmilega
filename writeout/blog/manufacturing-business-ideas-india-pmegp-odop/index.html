8 Small Manufacturing Business Ideas in India Under ₹20 Lakh (With PMEGP Subsidy and ODOP Clusters)

Eight manufacturing units a first-time founder can actually set up for ₹3–20 lakh, from areca leaf plates to corrugated boxes. Each one comes with machinery cost, break-even, matching ODOP districts and PMEGP/PMFME subsidy maths.

October 28, 2026 · 10 min read

The manufacturing business ideas that work best for a first-time founder with ₹2.5–20 lakh are simple units that turn a local raw material into something buyers need every month. Think areca leaf plates, cotton balls and swabs, corrugated boxes, fruit processing, spice grinding, wooden toys, paper bags and cold-pressed oil. Each of these fits under the PMEGP project limit of ₹50 lakh for manufacturing, where the subsidy is 15–35% of project cost. Most of them also match an existing ODOP (One District One Product) cluster, so raw material, trained workers and buyers are already close by.

Agar tumhare paas thodi savings hai aur ek district se connection hai, yeh page tumhare liye hai. Every idea below has a machinery cost, a break-even range, the districts where it already runs, and the scheme that fits it best.

Why “manufacturing business ideas” is trending in October 2026

On 24 October 2026, Google Trends showed searches for “manufacturing business ideas” up +60% in the business ideas category and +100% in the Manufacturing category over a 24-hour window. A few things in the news explain a lot of that interest:

  • Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme: the Union Cabinet approved it on 28 October 2026 (PIB release). It has a total outlay of ₹1,856.67 crore over eight years, FY2026-27 to FY2033-34, according to ThePrint and PolicyEdge. Of that, ₹1,130 crore goes to anchor manufacturers and turnover-linked incentives, and ₹105 crore helps makers get or renew certification from international sports federations. It also funds Sports Goods Manufacturing Zones, Centres of Excellence, skills training and export help. Small units will mostly benefit from money to upgrade shared facilities in existing sports goods clusters. Detailed guidelines and MSME eligibility rules have not been published yet. (One headline puts the figure at ₹857 crore. That is a typo.)
  • ₹10,000 crore SME Growth Fund: the Union Cabinet approved it in early October 2026, according to Business Standard. It is an equity fund for growth-stage manufacturing SMEs, so a first-time founder won't qualify on day one. It does show where policy money is heading.
  • Gujarat MSME Incentive Scheme 2026: it runs from 1 June 2026 to 31 May 2031 and offers 7% interest assistance on term loans. Women, manufacturing startups and first-generation entrepreneurs get extra support (KNN India).
  • UP ODOP marketing proposal: on 26 October 2026 the UP Small Industries Corporation proposed a scheme to sell ODOP products and ODOC (One District One Cuisine) foods through retail stores, Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit and Zepto, according to UNI. No budget has been given, and the proposal has not been confirmed as approved. UP founders can track it on the official ODOP UP portal.
  • Budget 2026-27: the Budget key features include a bigger electronics components scheme, textile parks and a Natural Fibre Scheme. With Diwali in early November, people are also looking for products they can make before the festive rush.

The 8 ideas, one by one

1. Areca leaf plates and bowls

A small unit needs 2–3 hydraulic double-die machines (about ₹1.5–3 lakh each), a washing tank, a drying yard and a shed. The sheath falls from the tree on its own, so raw material costs only ₹0.50–1 per piece where areca grows. Depending on size, plates sell wholesale for ₹2–5 each. Because single-use plastic is banned, caterers and cloud kitchens keep buying.

Export warning: the US FDA import alert on areca leaf dinnerware (IA 23-15, issued May 2025) is still in force, and Canada and Australia have similar restrictions (KNN India). The cost and margin figures here assume you sell in India or other non-US markets.

Karimganj in Assam already has an ODOP for exactly this product. Read the Karimganj areca leaf plate guide for local sourcing details.

Right for: founders in areca belts (Assam, Karnataka, Kerala, Arunachal). Skip if: you have to truck the sheaths in from far away, because freight eats the margin, or your plan depends on selling to the US.

2. Cotton balls, pads and swabs

An automatic ear-bud machine plus a cotton ball and pad line with packing costs about ₹8–18 lakh. Plastic-stick ear buds have been banned since 1 July 2022, so you'll need paper or bamboo sticks. Medical or surgical-grade absorbent cotton needs a drug manufacturing licence from your state drug controller. Cosmetic-grade products are easier to start with.

Sirsa in Haryana has this as its ODOP. See cotton balls and swabs in Sirsa.

Right for: people near cotton-ginning belts who already have pharmacy or salon distribution contacts. Skip if: you can't handle drug-licence compliance.

3. Corrugated boxes

A semi-automatic 3-ply/5-ply plant (single-face corrugator, sheet cutter, slotter, stitcher and a basic flexo printer) costs ₹12–20 lakh, including the shed setup. Margins are thin at 10–18%, but orders repeat every month from FMCG, pharma and e-commerce sellers. Your buyers usually pay on 30–60 day credit, so plan for working capital.

Raw material pressure in 2026: kraft paper prices rose about 25% between mid-July and early October 2026. The South India box makers' association (SICBMA) says the rise could reach 35% after the 1 October price revision (Corrugman). Kraft paper carries 18% GST but boxes only 5%, so unused input tax credit piles up and ties up working capital. South Indian box makers have announced a 20% price increase, and some small units are thinking about pausing production. Build price-revision clauses into your buyer agreements and budget for a bigger GST credit balance.

Sirmaur in Himachal, close to the Kala Amb industrial area, has packaging as its ODOP. See corrugated boxes in Sirmaur.

Right for: founders within 50 km of an industrial area. Skip if: you don't have 2–3 anchor buyers lined up before you buy machines.

4. Fruit-based food processing

A pickle, jelly or pulp unit with a pulper, steam kettle, sealing machine and a small cold room runs ₹6–15 lakh. You'll need an FSSAI licence through FoSCoS. Mango season is short, so plan a second product (lemon, tomato, amla) to keep the machines running the rest of the year.

Good clusters include East Godavari fruit products, Vizianagaram mango jelly and Durg tomato products.

Right for: people with farmer connections and a home-kitchen recipe that already sells. Skip if: you can't afford 6 months of inventory.

5. Spice grinding and packing

Lender listicles put spice grinding at ₹2–5 lakh for a pulveriser, sifter and pouch packing machine. This is the cheapest way into food manufacturing. Your edge comes from buying raw spice straight from the mandi at harvest. Guntur chillies and Begusarai chilli are both ODOP products, and turmeric clusters run across Arunachal.

Right for: first-timers with ₹3–5 lakh. Skip if: you plan to compete with national brands on supermarket shelves in year one. Start with local kirana stores and B2B hotels.

6. Wooden toys and lacquerware

A lathe-based unit (2–4 lathes, a lacquer setup, a drying area and finishing tools) costs ₹3–8 lakh. Margins of 30–40% are possible because GI-tagged handmade toys sell at a premium online and in gift hampers. The Toys Quality Control Order makes BIS certification compulsory for toys. Check your product's status and any artisan exemption on Manakonline before you scale.

Start with the Etikoppaka wooden toys guide for Anakapalli. Kondapalli (NTR district) and Sukma wooden craft are other options.

Right for: founders who can work with existing artisan families. Skip if: you want fully automated volume production.

7. Paper bags (and jute bags)

According to lender estimates, an automatic paper bag machine costs about ₹5–6 lakh. With printing and a shed, total setup comes to ₹7–12 lakh. Plastic bans keep demand steady from pharmacies, sweet shops and garment stores, and orders peak before Diwali. In Araria or Katihar, jute bags made from local fibre are the stronger version of this idea.

Right for: town-based founders with retail contacts. Skip if: your district already has 5+ bag units undercutting each other on price.

8. Cold-pressed (kachi ghani) oil

A wooden ghani or small expeller with a filter press, tanks and a bottle-filling setup costs ₹5–12 lakh. The oil cake left over sells as cattle feed, which adds 10–15% to revenue. You'll need FSSAI. Good clusters are Mahendragarh mustard oil and Junagadh or Amreli groundnut.

Right for: people in oilseed belts who can build a local brand. Skip if: you can't stock seed at harvest prices.

Festive side-bet: candles (₹10,000–3 lakh) and agarbatti (₹50,000–3 lakh) are low-cost options for Diwali. But demand is seasonal, so use them to test the market. Don't treat them as your main unit.

Paise kaise aayenge: PMEGP and PMFME subsidy for a manufacturing unit

PMEGP (via KVIC)

  • Maximum project cost: ₹50 lakh for manufacturing (₹20 lakh for service units). Some old state pages still show ₹25 lakh, which is out of date.
  • Subsidy: general category 15% urban / 25% rural, with a 10% own contribution. Special categories (SC/ST/OBC/women/minorities and others) get 25% / 35%, with a 5% own contribution.
  • The subsidy is not paid in cash. It sits in a 3-year term deposit linked to your loan and is adjusted after that.
  • Only new units qualify. A district committee (DLTFC) interviews you before your file goes to the bank. For manufacturing projects above ₹10 lakh, you must have passed class 8.

Figures released on 3 October 2026 show PMEGP has helped about 8.12 lakh units since FY2014-15, with ₹24,398.61 crore in margin money subsidy and about 73.08 lakh jobs (Punjab Kesari/IANS).

Example: take a ₹15 lakh areca plate unit in a rural area, applied for by a general-category founder. You put in ₹1.5 lakh, the bank lends ₹13.5 lakh, and ₹3.75 lakh (25%) of subsidy comes off your loan after 3 years. Apply on the KVIC PMEGP e-portal. The Ministry of MSME also runs pmegp.msme.gov.in, which some reports call a new PMEGP portal. Check which one is accepting applications before you fill the form, and ignore any third-party site asking for fees.

PMFME (food units only)

PMFME offers a 35% credit-linked capital subsidy, capped at ₹10 lakh per unit, with at least 10% of the project cost from you. It suits ideas 4, 5 and 8 and gives priority to ODOP products. You normally pick either PMFME or PMEGP for a project, not both.

Status as of 28 October 2026: the scheme's extended term ended on 30 September 2026. A 2026-31 continuation has been proposed, with a higher subsidy cap and priority for women and hill regions, but we found no Cabinet approval or notification of it yet. The scheme has met its target of sanctioning 2 lakh units, and about 41% of beneficiaries are women (Business Standard). Some third-party pages still say “Scheme Open”. Before you apply, check the official PMFME portal or ask your State Nodal Agency or District Resource Person. If PMFME is closed, PMEGP still covers food units.

Other options

Mudra loans go up to ₹20 lakh (Tarun Plus, for borrowers who repaid an earlier Tarun loan). CGTMSE covers collateral-free loans. Jan Samarth lists several government loan schemes in one place. If you're thinking about sports goods, wait for the guidelines of the Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme approved on 28 October 2026. Small units will mostly benefit through cluster facility upgrades.

Launch roadmap: 8 steps from idea to first invoice

  1. Pick your district's product. Match the idea to your district's ODOP so raw material and labour are local. You can browse districts on the kidharmilega homepage.
  2. Find 2–3 buyers first. Get letters of intent from caterers, pharmacies, FMCG units or exporters.
  3. Register on Udyam. It's free and Aadhaar-based on udyamregistration.gov.in. Ignore paid lookalike sites. Under the current limits, a micro enterprise is up to ₹2.5 crore investment and ₹10 crore turnover.
  4. Make a project report with three machinery quotes, a 3-year cash flow and a break-even month.
  5. Apply on PMEGP or PMFME (confirm PMFME is open first) and prepare for the DLTFC interview.
  6. Finish the EDP training that PMEGP requires before your loan is released.
  7. Get your licences: GST, FSSAI for food, BIS for toys or other QCO products, and pollution board consent or a factory licence if you need one.
  8. Run trials and sell locally first. Look at e-commerce and exports only after 3 months of consistent quality.

Frequently asked questions

Which manufacturing business can I start with low investment?

Spice grinding (₹2–5 lakh), areca leaf plates (₹3–8 lakh) and wooden toys (₹3–8 lakh) are the cheapest serious units. Candles and agarbatti start lower, from ₹10,000–50,000, but they are seasonal. Pick one that matches your district's ODOP product so raw material is cheap and nearby.

How much subsidy does PMEGP give for a manufacturing unit?

PMEGP covers manufacturing projects up to ₹50 lakh. General-category applicants get 15% (urban) or 25% (rural) and put in 10% themselves. Special categories get 25% or 35% and put in 5%. The subsidy is held in a 3-year term deposit linked to the loan, not paid as cash.

Is PMFME open for new applications?

It isn't clear. PMFME's extended term ended on 30 September 2026. A 2026-31 continuation with a higher subsidy cap has been proposed, but as of 28 October 2026 we found no Cabinet approval or notification. Check pmfme.mofpi.gov.in or ask your State Nodal Agency before applying. If it's closed, food units can still apply under PMEGP.

Is Udyam registration free?

Yes. Udyam registration is free, paperless and based on Aadhaar, and only the official portal udyamregistration.gov.in is authorised to do it. Many lookalike websites charge fees for the same registration. You don't need them, and the government has issued a notice warning against them.

Can a first-time founder get money from the ₹10,000 crore SME Growth Fund?

Not usually. The fund the Union Cabinet approved in October 2026 is an equity fund for growth-stage SMEs, mainly in manufacturing, that need capital to expand, adopt technology, export or make acquisitions. First-time founders should look at PMEGP, PMFME, Mudra and CGTMSE-backed loans instead.

Which licences do I need for a small manufacturing unit?

Most units need Udyam registration and GST. Food units need an FSSAI licence. Toys, some electronics and other products under Quality Control Orders need BIS certification. Larger units or polluting processes also need pollution control board consent and possibly a factory licence. Medical-grade cotton products need a state drug licence.
