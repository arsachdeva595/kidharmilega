IIPM Food Processing Unit Proposals: How to Bid for the Bengaluru RFP, Plus a PMFME Subsidy Plan for Your Own Unit

IIPM Bengaluru wants a Food Business Operator to run its incubator's food processing units, and Corrigendum-1 has extended the 25 October deadline. This guide covers how to bid, and how to plan your own micro unit with the PMFME 35% subsidy.

October 31, 2026 · 8 min read

Searching for IIPM food processing unit proposals? They are bids for one tender. The Indian Institute of Plantation Management, Bengaluru (IIPM) issued Request for Proposal No. IIPM/FP/LB/2026-27 on 13 October 2026. It wants a Food Business Operator (FBO) to run the food processing units at its MSME-ASPIRE Livelihood Business Incubator. The deadline has been extended by Corrigendum-1, dated 21 October 2026. The "25 October 2026" date you may have seen on aggregator sites is the old one. You can download the original RFP PDF or open IIPM's tenders page.

Yeh page tumhare liye hai if you're thinking of bidding. It's also for you if the tender made you think, "why not start my own unit back home?" For that, the second half covers the PMFME subsidy, what goes into a DPR, and sample costs.

TL;DR: Open the corrigendum and note the new submission date and time. Then read the RFP for eligibility, EMD/fees, the revenue-share or lease terms and the submission address. There is no online portal, so you submit the proposal the way the PDF tells you to. Questions can go to administration@iipmb.edu.in.

What the IIPM food processing unit proposals call actually is

First, the name confusion. IIPM Bengaluru (iipmb.edu.in) has nothing to do with the defunct Delhi business school also called "IIPM". The Bengaluru institute was set up by the Ministry of Commerce & Industry and is co-promoted by the Coffee, Tea, Rubber and Spices Boards.

Second, the scheme. MSME-ASPIRE stands for A Scheme for Promotion of Innovation, Rural Industries and Entrepreneurship. Under it, institutions run Livelihood Business Incubators (LBIs) with machinery where rural entrepreneurs can train and start working. IIPM already has the equipment. The PDF's filename, "machinery usage in IIPM", suggests the operator would run IIPM's existing machines instead of buying their own. Confirm that in the document.

Third, the deal. The RFP sets out who can apply, the EMD or fees, whether you pay a lease, rent or revenue share, how long the contract runs and how bids are scored. We couldn't extract those terms reliably, so we haven't guessed them here. Read them in the PDF.

Which food processing units are on offer?

Aggregators list five units:

  1. Fruit & vegetable juice processing
  2. Virgin coconut oil extraction
  3. Onion processing
  4. Value-added food processing & packing
  5. Community seed bank & processing

One version of the listing leaves out coconut oil. Check the final list, the capacities, and whether you can bid for more than one unit in the RFP itself.

Who should bid, and who should skip

  • Right for you if: you already sell juices, cold-pressed oils, dehydrated onion or packaged snacks in Karnataka. Then running IIPM's machines lets you add capacity without spending ₹10–25 lakh on new equipment. FPOs and SHGs with a ready supply of raw material are also a natural fit, if the RFP's eligibility rules allow them.
  • Skip it if: you have no buyers lined up yet, or you can't be in Bengaluru every day. A revenue-share contract on someone else's machines still needs you to sell the output every month. Paise kaise aayenge if there's no market?

How to submit an IIPM food processing unit proposal: step by step

  1. Get the new deadline. Open Corrigendum-1 and write down the extended date and time. Anything submitted after that is out.
  2. Read the RFP end to end. In the RFP PDF, mark the eligibility criteria, the documents asked for, the EMD amount and how to pay it, the contract period and the evaluation formula.
  3. Ask about a site visit. Inspect the machines, their capacity and the utilities before you quote anything. If the PDF doesn't mention a visit, email administration@iipmb.edu.in.
  4. Collect your papers. You'll usually need an FSSAI licence or registration, Udyam registration, GST, PAN, bank statements, and proof of past food business turnover. Follow the RFP's own checklist, not this general list.
  5. Write a short business plan for each unit. Cover where the raw material comes from, monthly throughput, your buyers, pricing and staffing. Use the DPR outline below even if the RFP asks for something shorter.
  6. Make your financial offer exactly in the RFP format. Whatever the commercial terms are (rent, revenue share or a per-unit charge), put the numbers where the PDF tells you to and don't add your own conditions.
  7. Submit and keep proof. Send the proposal to the address or by the method in the RFP, and keep the courier receipt or email acknowledgement. Check the tenders page regularly in case another corrigendum comes out.

Careful: IIPM posted several other tenders in October 2026, for digital displays, answer booklets, water-purifier maintenance, sports items and posters. The FBO RFP is the only one about food processing.

Not in Bengaluru? Start your own food processing unit with PMFME

Most of you won't run IIPM's machines. You want a unit in your own district. The usual route is the PM Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises (PMFME) scheme run by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI).

  • Individual units: a 35% credit-linked capital subsidy on the eligible project cost, up to ₹10 lakh per unit. In general you put in at least 10% of the project cost and a bank loan covers the rest. The subsidy is credited against the loan.
  • Groups (FPOs, SHGs, co-operatives): 35% for shared infrastructure, up to ₹3 crore.
  • ODOP focus: new units making the district's One District One Product get priority.
  • Free DPR help: District Resource Persons (DRPs) appointed under the scheme help applicants prepare DPRs and loan files.

Status check, 31 October 2026: PMFME has reached its target of 2 lakh units, with about ₹19,844 crore of projects and about ₹5,844 crore of subsidy, according to Outlook Business. It was temporarily extended to 30 September 2026. A five-year "PMFME 2.0" (2026-31) has been proposed, but we have found no confirmation that it has been approved. Before you build a plan around the subsidy, ask your district industries centre or state PMFME nodal agency whether they are accepting new applications. Larger, ministry-level schemes under PMKSY are announced on MoFPI's Expressions of Interest page, and applicants apply through the SAMPADA portal. We found no open call for the unit scheme as of this date.

What goes into a food processing unit DPR

  1. Promoter profile: your experience, education, and the money you're putting in.
  2. Product and market: what you'll make, who buys it, competitor prices and your selling price.
  3. Raw material plan: where it comes from, the season, price per kg and storage.
  4. Process flow and capacity: each step, kg per hour, working days and capacity use (assume 50–60% in year 1, not 100%).
  5. Machinery list with quotations: at least one supplier quotation for each machine.
  6. Civil work, utilities and FSSAI layout: shed, power load, water and the hygiene zones.
  7. Project cost and means of finance: your contribution, the bank loan and the subsidy.
  8. Five-year projected P&L, cash flow and balance sheet, along with DSCR and the breakeven point.
  9. Approvals: Udyam, FSSAI, GST, and pollution consent if needed.

The cap is easy to check. A project costing ₹28.6 lakh or more hits the ₹10 lakh limit, so a ₹40 lakh project still gets only ₹10 lakh. Small seasonal fruit units often work out somewhere in the 15–25% gross margin range. That's a planning assumption, not a promise. With capacity use climbing from about 50%, breakeven in roughly 18–30 months is a sensible range to model in the DPR. If the bank's DSCR is below 1.5, cut the loan or the machine list.

Scope of food processing industry in India: the numbers

This section is for founders checking the demand side and for UPSC aspirants writing the "scope" answer:

  • Size: sector GVA was ₹1.92 lakh crore in 2022-23, growing about 5.35% a year, with 20.68 lakh people employed in 2021-22 (MoFPI data cited by Vision IAS). That is only about 1.7–1.8% of India's GVA. Grant Thornton Bharat says it needs to reach about 7.2% by 2047.
  • Jobs: 12.38% of registered manufacturing employment (Economic Survey 2021-22).
  • Exports: processed food exports were US$7,886.62 million in FY2024-25, more than 20% of agri-food exports.
  • FDI: US$15.856 billion cumulative from April 2000 to December 2025.
  • Budget: MoFPI got ₹4,064 crore for FY2026-27, up 13.79% from the 2025-26 revised estimate of ₹3,571.57 crore.
  • PLI (PLISFPI): outlay of ₹10,900 crore for FY2021-22 to 2026-27. So far ₹9,207 crore has been invested across 128 companies and 274 units, adding about 3.4 MMT a year of capacity and about 3.29 lakh jobs. Sales of supported products grew at a 10.58% compound annual rate (as of April 2026).

What this means for a small founder: processing levels are low and government support keeps coming, so there's room to grow. But margins come from sourcing raw material well and selling it well, not from the machines. Pick a crop your district already grows in surplus, keep the first unit small, and prove you have buyers before you scale.

Bottom line

If you want an IIPM unit, get the extended deadline from the corrigendum and all the terms from the RFP, and only then put numbers in your bid. If you want your own unit, find your DRP, write the DPR properly, and confirm PMFME's status before you take a loan. Jaldi karo, lekin andhe mein mat karo.

Frequently asked questions

Is the IIPM food processing unit proposal call still open?

IIPM Bengaluru extended the deadline for RFP No. IIPM/FP/LB/2026-27 through Corrigendum-1, dated 21 October 2026. The 25 October 2026 date on aggregator sites is the original deadline. Open the corrigendum PDF on iipmb.edu.in to see the exact new date and time, and check the tenders page in case another notice is posted.

Is IIPM Bengaluru the same as the Delhi IIPM business school?

No. The Indian Institute of Plantation Management, Bengaluru (iipmb.edu.in) was set up by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry and is co-promoted by the Coffee, Tea, Rubber and Spices Boards. The defunct Delhi business school called IIPM is a separate institution. The food processing RFP comes only from IIPM Bengaluru.

Which food processing units does IIPM want an operator for?

Aggregator listings name fruit and vegetable juice processing, virgin coconut oil extraction, onion processing, value-added food processing and packing, and a community seed bank with processing. One version leaves out coconut oil. The RFP PDF is the final word on which units are offered, their capacities and whether you can bid for more than one.

What subsidy can I get to start my own food processing unit?

Under PMFME, individual micro units can get a 35% credit-linked subsidy on the eligible project cost, up to ₹10 lakh. FPOs, SHGs and co-operatives can get 35% up to ₹3 crore for shared infrastructure. The scheme was extended to 30 September 2026, and PMFME 2.0 has been proposed but is not yet confirmed. Check with your district nodal agency before you apply.

What should a food processing unit DPR contain?

A bankable DPR covers the promoter profile, the product and its market, the raw material plan, process flow and capacity, a machinery list with supplier quotations, civil work and utilities, the project cost and how it is financed, five-year financial projections with DSCR and breakeven, and the licences you need, such as FSSAI, Udyam and GST. PMFME District Resource Persons help prepare it free.
